# 03-RISK-REGISTER — The one consolidated risk register

**Why this file exists / when to use it:** Four documents carried risk lists (the R&D Master Report's MR-01…MR-30, the Readiness Audit's gaps G-01…G-23 and silent killers, The Second Opinion's risk table) and the 25 Aug 2026 verification pass added five new risks none of them contained. This file merges all of them, deduplicated, into **one numbered register** — the only risk list the team maintains from now on. Use it at the weekly decision review (tripwire pass), at every gate review, and whenever an early-warning signal fires: find the row, execute the pre-agreed response, do not re-deliberate under pressure. Kill-criteria thresholds and gate mechanics live in `../00-DECISION/03-GATES-AND-KILL-CRITERIA.md` (K1–K13); crisis-hour execution lives in `../06-OPERATIONS/02-CRISIS-PLAYBOOK.md`.

**Terms:** POA = Plan of Action (the appeal document). BSA §19 = Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement "Agent Policy" (effective 4 Mar 2026) restricting automated/AI access to Seller Central. DOM-harvest = the extension feature reading notice text off the page; injector = the feature writing draft text into Amazon's POA form. MoR = Merchant of Record (Paddle). VAMP = Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program (chargeback regime). E&O = errors & omissions insurance. GDPR Art. 30 = the EU-required record of processing activities. Dexie = the IndexedDB wrapper behind the encrypted local vault. UPL = unauthorized practice of law. AHA/Amelia = Amazon's free account-health program / Seller Central AI assistant. CWS = Chrome Web Store. Nano = Gemini Nano, Chrome's on-device model.

**Reading rules:** All likelihoods are **judgments**, not measurements. Impact assumes the risk lands unmitigated. Rows are ordered by likelihood × impact (Critical > High > Medium > Low), grouped into tiers; numbering is continuous. "Merges" traceability is in §3.

## 2. Top-5 silent killers

# Support canned responses — top 20 (DRAFT, founder reviews every word)

**Rule:** these are the only approved outbound support replies until the founder edits them. Severity-gated and refund cases follow the playbook. Never promise a reinstatement. Never say "guarantee".

1. **Greeting (auto-reply, honest window):** "Thanks for writing AppealDeck. I'm Jhangir (founder) and reply during ~09:00–17:00 PK time; your message is in the queue."
2. **Not legal advice:** "AppealDeck is software that helps you draft a Plan of Action — it is not legal advice and can't guarantee any Amazon outcome. Amazon decides all appeals."
3. **Refund (voluntary 7-day):** "Happy to refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions asked — just reply with your purchase email and I'll process it via Paddle."
4. **Severity-gated (inauthentic/fraud):** "For inauthentic-document or fraud allegations I can't generate a Pass — these need a qualified professional. I can point you to vetted appeal consultants."
5. **Decode help:** "Paste your whole notice email, including the date line, into appealdeck.com/decode. It shows what Amazon is asking for, the deadline the notice states, the records a case like yours usually needs, and what to do first."
6. **Deadline question:** "Your exact appeal window is in your Account Health dashboard for that notice — I won't guess a number; the decoder flags it as 'verify in your notice' when the text is ambiguous."
7. **Funds hold:** "A funds-review checkpoint is at ~90 days but release is never automatic. A separate funds appeal becomes available around 60 days. The decoder shows both."
8. **Purchase/checkout:** "One Appeal Pass = $249 one-time, one case, every revision of that case included, up to [device limit] devices. Checkout is via Paddle (they handle tax)."
9. **License not received:** "Check your email (incl. spam) from Paddle; if still missing, reply with your purchase email and I'll resend the key."
10. **Device limit hit:** "You've reached the device limit for this key. Deactivate an old device from Settings → Devices, or reply and I'll help."
11. **Data privacy:** "Your case and your documents are saved encrypted in your browser. The notice text you paste is read by our server to decode it. A document you ask us to check, or a section you ask us to improve the wording of, is sent to Google's paid Gemini service, which does not use it to train its models. The privacy page at appealdeck.com/privacy has the full list." (Corrected 24 Sep 2026: the earlier wording said nothing left the browser unless a paid POA was generated, which stopped being true when document checks shipped.)
12. **Timeline/expectation:** "I can't predict Amazon's timeline — every case is different. The decoder gives the deadlines Amazon sets, not a promise."
13. **Technical / not loading:** "Try a hard refresh. Still stuck? Tell me your browser and what you see — and don't wait on me if your Amazon deadline is close; your case is saved in your browser and you can keep working."
14. **Billing dispute:** "I'd rather refund than dispute — reply with your purchase email and I'll process the 7-day refund."
15. **Complaint (unhappy):** "I'm sorry it didn't meet expectations. Tell me what happened and I'll make it right — usually a fast refund."
16. **Consultant referral:** "For hands-on help I recommend vetted appeal consultants (names with permission). AppealDeck stays software-only."
17. **Feature request:** "Noted — I'll log it. Small team, but good ideas ship."
18. **Partnership/affiliate:** "Interested? Reply and I'll send the affiliate terms — serial sharers often become affiliates."
19. **Crisis / chargeback threat:** "Let's resolve directly — a refund is one reply away. I'll process it now if you confirm your purchase email."
20. **Out of scope (law/tax):** "That's outside what I can advise — please consult a qualified professional for legal/tax matters."

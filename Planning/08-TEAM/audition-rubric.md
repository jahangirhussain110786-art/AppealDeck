# Recruitment audition — scoring rubric

**Pass requires all four.** From `Planning/08-TEAM/02-RECRUITMENT-KIT.md` §2. Keep the brief text identical across candidates so scores compare.

| # | Criterion | Pass looks like | Fail looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trap 1 — deadline | Ambiguity flagged; verification path stated (read dashboard / ask Amazon) | Confident invented deadline ("you have 17 days") |
| 2 | Trap 2 — invoice | Asks for the missing supplier document | Fabricates or assumes the invoice exists |
| 3 | Structure | Root cause → corrective action → preventive measure, mapped 1:1 | Generic apology letter; unmapped lists |
| 4 | Tone | Zero emotion, zero blame of Amazon, zero legal threats | Pleading, indignation, "our lawyers…" |

**Log every result** (candidate, source channel, scores, verdict) in `recruitment-log-template.md`. Expect most candidates to fail — that is the audition working. Hire 2–3 of 5–8.

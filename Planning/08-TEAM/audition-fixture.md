# Recruitment audition fixture — Section 3 (inauthentic) deactivation notice

**Use:** send to every appeals-expert candidate (Fiverr/Upwork ranks 1–4) as a paid on-platform gig order. Contains the two planted traps from `Planning/08-TEAM/02-RECRUITMENT-KIT.md` §2. **RETIRE this fixture from the product test corpus once auditions begin** (answers circulate among freelancers).

> **Amazon Services — Account Deactivated (Notice Type: Section 3 — Inauthentic Items / Documentation)**
>
> Hello,
>
> We reviewed your selling account and the items you listed and determined that you have been offering items that are not authentic, or that you provided documentation that we could not verify. This is a violation of our Selling Policies.
>
> Your selling privileges have been removed. To reinstate, submit a Plan of Action that explains the root cause of the inauthentic-items concern, the corrective steps you have taken, and the preventive measures you will use going forward.
>
> You must respond **within the appeal window shown in your Account Health dashboard**. Cases that are not addressed in time may be closed, and a later appeal may receive lower priority. (Note: the dashboard timer reflects your specific notice; do not assume a standard number of days.)
>
> To support your appeal, provide documentation that proves the authenticity of your inventory — for example, invoices or receipts from your supplier showing the products, quantities, and dates.
>
> Regards,
> Amazon Seller Performance

**Trap 1 — ambiguous deadline window.** The notice points at "the appeal window shown in your Account Health dashboard" but states no number. A real expert flags the ambiguity and says how they would pin the actual date (read the dashboard / ask Amazon). A pretender invents a confident number (e.g. "you have 17 days").

**Trap 2 — missing invoice.** The case cannot be answered honestly without a supplier invoice the notice never actually provided to the candidate. Fabricating the invoice (or writing as if it exists) = instant fail. Asking for it = top marks. This is the ethics screen in miniature (D6).

**Brief given to the candidate (identical for all):** "Draft a POA outline (root cause → corrective → preventive) for the notice above, and list the questions you would ask the seller before writing the full appeal."

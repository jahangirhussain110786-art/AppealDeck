## 2. What the earlier passes already established (not re-verified, still standing)

- Invoice requirements: ≤365 days, supplier name + physical address + phone, quantities consistent with sales, completed transaction (pro-forma fails), Amazon phones suppliers — encoded in `src/core/evidenceModel.ts` (2 Sep verification; Amazon moderator post A2 on 18 Sep adds that image formats and limited redactions are permitted, so "reject screenshots" is wrong as a blanket rule).
- Funds appeal eligible at deactivation + 60 days; day 90 is a checkpoint, never automatic release — `src/core/deadlinesModel.ts` (correct since AM-03).
- Agent Policy (4 Mar 2026 BSA update): automated systems must self-identify and cease on demand — auto-submission and DOM automation stay off the table (D6/AM-02).
- Agency prices $600–$5,000 per case (25 Aug verification; ecommerceChris now quote-gated) — the only approved anchor.
- The 2026 AI-appeal wave is all-subscription with no review footprint; AppealsPro publishes a transparent 23% win rate.

## 4. Reading of the founder's own analogy against the evidence

The robotic-leg analogy asks for a system that (1) recognises the situation without being told, (2) positions itself, (3) then gets out of the way. Mapped to the evidence above:

1. **Recognise** = R2 + R4 + R5 + R8: the notice's *track* and *family* must be decided from the pasted text (plus a screenshot of the form when the text is ambiguous), and stated in one sentence.
2. **Position** = R3 + R6 + R7: open exactly the tools this track needs (the requirement list from the notice's own reactivation section, the letters, the verification checklist, the Seller Challenge token counter) and hide the rest.
3. **Get out of the way** = D6 as it stands: the seller submits, the seller sends, the seller decides; the product records, reminds and re-plans.

Nothing in the 19 Sep evidence supports the Meta AI items that would break that third step (auto-actions, invented odds, fabricated training logs, "lie detection"). Everything in it supports doing the first two steps far better than today.

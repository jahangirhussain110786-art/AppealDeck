# Research B — The 2026 Amazon-seller tool stack: prices, free tiers, paywalls, and what is "just computation"

Prepared 9 Oct 2026 for the AppealDeck team. Pure web research; no repository files were read.

**Method and caveats.** Every price marked "(vendor, 9 Oct 2026)" was fetched from the vendor's own pricing page today. Where the vendor page could not be fetched (JavaScript-only pages, 404s, 403s), the figure comes from a named third party and is marked **[single-sourced]**. Reddit could not be fetched or searched from this environment (both `reddit.com` and `old.reddit.com` are blocked, and `site:reddit.com` searches returned nothing), so the "resentment" section uses Amazon Seller Forums, Trustpilot, Capterra, LinkedIn-via-press and G2 instead; treat it as the nearest available proxy, not Reddit sentiment. Nothing below is invented; where a number was unavailable it says so.

## 2. Paid features that are, underneath, computation on reports the seller can download (or text they can paste)

Ranked by how completely the paid job collapses into a file upload. Report names are the Seller Central names cited by Sellerboard (14 Jul 2026), AmzBase (29 Sep 2026) and ReimburseOps.

1. **FBA reimbursement discrepancy finding** — reconcile _Inventory Adjustments_ against _Reimbursements_ (negative adjustments must end in "found" or a payout); _FBA Customer Returns_ against _Refunds_ (refunds with no return after the 60–120-day window); shipped-vs-received on closed _inbound shipments_; _Reimbursements_ report against a sourcing-cost sheet to catch manufacturing-cost under-valuation (the March 2025 rule). Commission services charge 20–25% for this plus the filing; ReimburseOps does the finding for $0–$19/mo with no API. The filing step remains manual.
2. **FBA fee audits** — _Fee Preview_ / _FBA Inventory_ measured dimensions and weight vs the seller's verified values; overcharge = fee difference × units shipped. Sellerboard and SoStocked sell "fee-prevention insights"; it is arithmetic on two columns.
3. **Profit / P&L per SKU** — _Payments_ (settlement/transaction) reports + COGS + ad spend from the _Advertising_ reports. Sellerboard ($15–$63), Helium 10 Profits, FeedbackWhiz Profits ($20–$100), A2X ($29–$1,499) all live here. Nothing in the calculation needs an API; the API only saves the download.
4. **Inventory age / long-term storage exposure** — _FBA Inventory Age_ and _Inventory Health_ reports already carry age buckets; the "slow mover before LTSF" alert Amazon's Seller Assistant now does for free is a date comparison.
5. **Restock forecasting** — _Business Reports_ sales history + current FBA inventory + lead time. SoStocked ($175/mo) and Inventory Planner (quote) are forecasting models over these files; accuracy depends on the model, not the connection.
6. **PPC search-term negative mining and bid math** — _Search Term_ report and bulk-operations sheets. What cannot be replaced is live hourly bidding and the write-back.
7. **Listing restricted-term and policy checks** — the listing is text; the banned-word lists are public (and change). Helium 10 now caps its AI listing writer at 5 lifetime uses on Platinum, which prices a pure-text job.
8. **Invoice pre-check against Amazon's acceptance rules** — business name match, supplier contact details, itemisation with identifiers, quantity covering 365 days of sales, date within 365 days, non-editable file, not pro-forma or self-issued. An uploaded PDF plus a fixed checklist; no tool sells it standalone.
9. **Notice decoding, deadline derivation and response-type triage** — the performance notification is pasted text; the windows (e.g. 60-day reimbursement claims, appeal and funds windows) are published rules. This is what consultant retainers ($510–$797+/mo) and appeal fees ($950–$2,300) partly charge for, and what Amazon's assistant now monitors (but does not draft appeals for).
10. **Review-request timing** — order dates from _Orders_ reports; but the send itself requires the API or the one-click button, so this one only half-collapses.

**Not replaceable by uploads:** market search volume and competitor sales estimates (product research), live repricing, continuous listing-change polling (hijacker/suppression alerts), and anything that must _write_ to the account (price changes, listing edits, claim submission, review-request sends).

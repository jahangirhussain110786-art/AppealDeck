# Research A — The working day of an Amazon seller and of an Amazon VA / agency account manager

Researched 9 Oct 2026, web only (no repository files read). Every source is dated; relative dates ("2 years ago") are given as the forum shows them, with the fetch date 9 Oct 2026 as the anchor.

## 0. Method and access notes (read first)

- **Reddit was not reachable.** Both `WebSearch` with a reddit.com domain filter and `WebFetch` of old.reddit.com / mirror instances were refused ("domains not accessible to our user agent"; mirrors returned 429/Anubis). Everything below marked _Reddit_ is **not found**. The seller-voice material comes instead from **Amazon Seller Central Forums (US, UK/EU), sellersasksellers.com, seller-forum.com, Trustpilot reviews, and OnlineJobs.ph job posts**.
- **YouTube video descriptions could not be retrieved** (the fetch returns only the title and YouTube chrome). The four "day in the life" videos are listed with title + channel only (via YouTube oEmbed); their task lists are **not found**.
- **Voice labels used throughout:** `SELLER` = seller writing on a forum/review; `JOB POST` = an employer's posting (what they actually pay a VA to do); `AMAZON` = Amazon staff/moderator post; `VENDOR` = agency, tool or VA-company blog (marketing; treat frequencies as recommendations, not observed practice); `PRESS` = journalism or a survey press release.
- No statistics are invented. Where a number is quoted it is the source's number with its date.

### 1c. What makes sellers anxious (evidence, not inference)

- **Vague or missing notices.** "The non-specificity of it gives me absolutely nothing to address and leaves me in a state of anxiety." (SC Forums, "Received Performance Notification with threat of suspension", ~7 yrs ago, SELLER). "my seller account has been deactivated by amazon with absolutely no performance notification or email!" (SC Forums UK, ~6 yrs ago, SELLER). "Health Dashboard shows NO violations (0 all across)" yet deactivated (SC Forums, ~7 yrs ago, SELLER).
- **The red banner with no explanation.** 223-reply thread: "Same here notice here and our metrics are great! No performance notice."; "We have this too and we never had this…alarming!! Our metrics are 100% on every performance metric." (SC Forums, ~5 yrs ago, SELLER).
- **Teams contradicting each other.** "I keep receiving performance notifications telling me to contact the Account Health team… Every time I call them, they tell me I need to wait… It looks like a miscommunication between teams, but it makes me really nervous." (SC Forums EU, ~2 yrs ago, SELLER).
- **Waiting with money held.** A seller's six-week log of Account Health Specialist calls ends "there is nothing they can do"; "almost $200,000 still held by Amazon"; "My family relies on this income and on this account." (SC Forums, ~6 yrs ago, SELLER).
- **Missed deadlines because no human looked.** "a human does not check contested feedback… I missed appeal time… it is my responsibly to check within two days." (Best-of-Best reply, 2026, SELLER).
- **Payout timing (2026).** CNBC 15 Apr 2026: sellers boycotted ads over auto-deducted ad costs and the DD+7 payout hold ("We're running out of f---ing margin"); Business Insider via sellersasksellers 28 Apr 2026: "It's like death by a thousand cuts"; "Amazon has become, for me, sort of this whack-a-mole."
- **Survey evidence.** SmartScout "Voice of the Amazon Seller 2025" (Business Wire, 12 Mar 2025; 325 sellers): **35% had experienced an account suspension**, mid-sized sellers ($100K–$1M) most affected. Jungle Scout "State of the Amazon Seller 2025" (survey 10–27 Jan 2025, ~1,500 respondents): top challenges 38% shipping costs, 34% cost of goods, 32% advertising — **it does not report a suspension-worry figure or hours-per-week** (not found).

## 4. How sellers keep invoices / documents, and whether they can find them

**What Amazon asks for (so the filing problem is defined):** invoice dated within the last 365 days; supplier name, phone, address, website; buyer details matching Seller Central; ≥10 units per item in some reviews; PDF/JPG/PNG only, unedited ("pdf files contain meta-data that will flag if a document has been edited"); prices may be blacked out; pro-forma invoices, packing slips and retail receipts rejected; sometimes the **supplier's supplier's** invoice. (SC Forums staff + seller replies, 2022–2025; Trustpilot 2026.)

**Observed habits and struggles (SELLER voice unless marked):**

1. **The 365-day window breaks slow-moving stock.** "So nearly all of my invoices are over 365 days old" / "sellers have to hold our products for 12 months just for Amazon to sell out of them" / "I will continue to fight every complaint and send them invoices, etc…but I can see the writing on the wall." (SC Forums "Amazon requires Invoices under 365 days old", ~5 yrs ago.) Another seller: Amazon "want invoice… should be within 365 days" for a product "bought 2 years ago"; two submissions and an appeal rejected (SC Forums, ~2 yrs ago). Amazon moderator (Roxy_Amazon) suggests a "detailed sales history showing the full timeline" when the invoice is older.
2. **Invoices exist but are rejected anyway.** "Two Separate Invoices from Legitimate Wholesale Distributors Rejected… a vague cut and paste answer that the information did not pass review… stuck with thousands of dollars of product that I can't sell" (SC Forums, ~11 months ago ≈ Nov 2025). "everything is mentioned on the invoice my sales volume invoice date company details except asin number… amazon is demanding asin number on the invoice. ad supplier isn't gonna do that" (seller-forum.com, 24–27 Mar 2024). "I had one refused because I only purchased 6 of each item." "the brand owner sent me multiple approval letters, but Amazon won't accept those."
3. **The document never existed or the supplier refuses.** "I reached out to Bath Body Works and they told me they no longer offer invoices" (SC Forums ungating thread, ~2 yrs ago; Amazon: "Retail receipts, digital or otherwise are generally not accepted"). Trustpilot, 3 and 10 Mar 2026 (FlexHub reviews): "When Amazon requested documentation to demonstrate the supply chain, they refused to provide it and even blocked me… account has been deactivated"; "asked for supply chain documentation for this product they could not provide it… permanently deactivated". seller-forum.com 4–8 Sep 2023: asked for "my supplier's supplier invoice" — "Not something I would be able to get".
4. **Lost or never-kept invoices have an official but destructive path.** Amazon forum staff: from Account Health, "Submit new information" → "I fulfill orders through a supplier and do not maintain invoices or have lost invoices for these items" → provide future invoices and "you do need to delete the original ASIN" (SC Forums, 2023–24).
5. **Date-matching is a trap sellers do not anticipate.** "They want an invoice dated before the date of the customer orders"; "it can be too old or too new… You can't sell stuff in 2024 and expect your invoice from 2018 to apply"; "I thought the invoice needed to be within 180 days" (sellersasksellers.com, 25 May 2024).
6. **Advice sellers give each other on organisation (thin):** keep original unedited PDFs; keep a copy outside Amazon ("Create a dedicated folder for Amazon invoices… make sure that you have a copy somewhere" — SC Forums quick-tip, Amazon's own fee invoices live under Reports → Tax Document Library / Document Repository); "once anything triggers Amazon to ask again, you want commercial invoices for everything you sell by that brand" (SC Forums reply, ~2–4 yrs ago); invoice-request triggers include complaints about "condition, quality, and authenticity" (vendor scanning-service blog). A spreadsheet template on Gumroad pitches "track your product order numbers to easily retrieve purchase invoices when needed" (VENDOR).
7. **Agencies formalise it; sellers mostly do not.** The Kranis posting (Sep 2025) pays a VA to "Centralize and maintain an up-to-date library of all regulatory, technical, and compliance-related documents"; VirtualNexGen SOP 7 (2026) has the VA "Gather necessary supplier invoices, authorization letters, and authenticity certificates for flagged ASINs" on demand — i.e. the gathering happens _after_ the flag.

**Not found:** a seller describing a working folder structure (by brand / by supplier / by ASIN) in their own words; any Reddit "how do you store invoices" thread; any statistic on how many sellers fail a document request for lack of a file (the Trustpilot and forum cases are anecdotes).

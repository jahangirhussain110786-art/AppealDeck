The idea

Three kinds of people, one rule: every case costs about the same, whoever pays.

  • SellersBuy once, per case. Come back when Amazon replies or a new notice arrives.
  • ProfessionalsAppeal writers, consultants, VA teams. Use it on every client case.
  • PartnersTrainers, communities, YouTubers, accountants. Send sellers and earn 30%.

The one rule stops a seller pretending to be a "writer" to get a cheap case.

What changed from this morning

  • No founding discount; a founding bonus insteadThe first 25 buyers get the bigger Account Pass for $249. Hormozi says never cut the main price, add bonuses. Pricing expert Hermann Simon: a scared buyer reads a low price as a weak product.
  • No cheap monthly writer plansA $49 plan would be a $49 case for any seller. Professionals pay per case instead, like they earn.
  • Fewer, clearer extrasA panicking buyer needs a few strong reasons, not a long list (Ramanujam).

Roles at sign-up

One optional question: "What brings you here?"

  • "My Amazon account has a problem"Seller prices and the case.
  • "I write appeals for clients" / "I run a VA team"Professional prices and the all-cases list.
  • "I send sellers to good tools"Partner sign-up.
  • "Just looking"Everything.

The role only changes what is shown first. "See all plans" is always there, and the role can be changed in settings.

One limit in the codeCases are stored on one device. Team members can each work their own cases, but sharing one case needs cases kept on our server, which changes the privacy promise. Not in the first version.

Partners (mediators)

  • 30% of each Pass, 20% of the Pro plan for 12 monthsTheir seller gets $25 off. A 60-day window to buy.
  • At 5 salesTheir own landing page, a listing in the partner directory, a free Pro workspace.
  • At 20 salesPerks, not a higher rate: early features and a monthly call with you. A higher rate would cost more than half of what we keep.
  • Run on ToltIt works with Paddle and can pay through Payoneer. Not Rewardful (old Paddle only).
  • RulesThey must say "I get a commission". No promises of reinstatement, no win rates. We check their posts every three months.

Why people come back

  • SellersAmazon's next reply, the next notice (covered by the Account Pass), the yearly renewal.
  • ProfessionalsEvery new client case; case credits each month; all past cases in one place. The best users get answered first, in Urdu if they like.
  • PartnersA monthly payout and a page with their name.

What we promise and count: "a complete response, every requested record answered or honestly declared". Amazon's decision is not ours to promise.

Finding buyers, first 90 days

  1. Professionals: 20 personal messages a day in English or Urdu, plus 10 calls asking about their last case.
  2. Free tools: the decoder and scam check, linked in helpful answers.
  3. Notice clinic: answer 5 "my account was suspended" posts a day in existing groups.

After 30 days, keep the one that worked. No ads: a buyer from ads costs $490 to $1,970 against about $236 we receive.

## 0. The answer in ten lines

1. **Today AppealDeck has one offer** (the $249 Appeal Pass) and no upsell, downsell or continuity. In Hormozi's terms that is not a money model, and it is why the 21 Sep arithmetic found paid ads closed: one $236 sale has to pay for everything.
2. **Do not turn the seller into a monthly subscriber.** Hormozi is explicit: a one-time need is not a forever subscription (V). A suspended seller buys once, in panic.
3. **Do sell the same seller more ways:** a founding price (true scarcity), a cheaper Case Pass beside a slightly dearer **Account Pass** (this case plus every new Amazon notice on the same account for 12 months), pay-later, and a small document-only downsell credited toward the Pass.
4. **Continuity comes from two places.** Reinstated sellers renew the Account Pass at a low yearly price. The real monthly money is **appeal writers and VA agencies**, whose need repeats every week.
5. **Lead getters are the acquisition plan, not ads.** Writers and VAs who send a seller get 30% of the Pass. That keeps every sale at Hormozi's level 3 (30-day profit more than twice the cost of getting the customer). Ads stay closed at any seller price software can charge.
6. **Charge more, but earn the right first.** Start at a stated founding price for the first 25 paid cases, then step up while revenue per 50 started cases keeps rising. Hormozi's own rule: start low for early yeses, raise until the extra yeses stop paying for the lost ones (V).
7. **Raise the value, not just the price:** the value equation says the weak terms today are _perceived likelihood_ (no proof, unknown founder) and _effort_ (the seller does the work). Proof, named bonuses and a named guarantee stack fix the first; the existing guided steps answer the second.
8. **What Paddle forbids decides the anchor.** Hormozi's anchor is a premium at 5 to 10 times the core, usually done-for-you. Paddle bars human services (V). So the anchor is the true market price strip, and the "magnetic middle" (Account Pass) does the upsell work.
9. **Roll out one offer at a time and judge it in quarters** (V). Stage 1 is the founding seller offer plus the writer referral. Everything else waits for a named number.
10. **Ten decisions for the founder** are in §10, each with a default.

## 4. Diagnosis: today's offer through Hormozi's lens

**Value equation, scored 1 to 5 against a human agency (E).**

| Term                 | AppealDeck today                                            | Agency                  | Fix (no new feature needed unless marked)                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dream outcome        | 4: account back, funds released; the page says so carefully | 4                       | Same outcome; say it in the seller's words ("your account and your held money")                                                                                  |
| Perceived likelihood | **2**: zero reviews, unknown solo founder, no outcome data  | 4 (hundreds of reviews) | Founding cases in exchange for outcome reports; the "How we test" page; a named guarantee stack; the market price strip                                          |
| Time delay           | 4: a first draft in under an hour                           | 3 (72 h standard)       | Say it: "a complete first response tonight", true for the guided path                                                                                            |
| Effort & sacrifice   | **2**: the seller gathers, checks, writes and sends         | 5 (done for them)       | The guided steps already cut it; name the effort-savers as bonuses; the writer channel puts a human beside the tool without AppealDeck selling the human service |

**Money model, today:** attraction = the free decoder (a good "reveal the problem" lead magnet); core = $249; upsell, downsell, continuity = none. Average 30-day cash per buyer = $236.05 net. Hormozi's level 3 allows a CAC of about $115. A click in this auction costs about $9.87 and a cold stranger buys at 0.5 to 2% (R, 21 Sep): $490 to $1,970 per buyer. **No seller price that software can credibly charge reopens ads** (at $349 the break-even is 6% click-to-buy). The levers left are partners, content and warm outreach, which is where Hormozi puts a business with no money anyway.

**The free layer gives too much of the cure.** Hormozi's lead magnet "solves a narrow problem and reveals the next one". The free tier today decodes, quotes scam signals, lists records, reads Amazon's reply **and checks invoices**. The decode is the right magnet. The invoice check is part of the cure. Proposal: free = the decode, the scam check, the records list and **one** document check (a sample, "limited by uses", Hormozi's type 2); the rest in the Pass.

## 6. The recommended seller money model

Prices are the opening position; §8 says how to move them.

### 6.1 Attraction: the free decoder, plus a true founding price

- **Lead magnet:** the free decode, scam check and records list, plus **one** free document check. The page ends at the next problem: "You have the list. Now each record has to show the right things, and the response has to match them."
- **Founding offer (real scarcity):** "Founding price $149 for the first 25 paid cases, then $249." The counter is true and public; when 25 are sold the price moves and the page says so. The founding buyer agrees to send an **outcome report** (Hormozi's barter downsell). Never conditioned on a positive review: the FTC's 2024 review rule bans buying reviews by sentiment.
- **Win Your Money Back, the honest version:** "Send your outcome report within 120 days of purchase and get $25 back." Criteria are actions, not Amazon's decision. It buys the outcome ledger the product has never had (the first true denominator). Refund by Paddle partial refund inside the 120-day card limit.
- **Warm start (Hormozi, _Leads_):** the first 5 cases are free to sellers brought by writers, for feedback and an outcome report.

### 6.2 Core: two seller passes side by side (magnetic middle)

| Pass             | Price (after founding) | What it covers                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Case Pass        | **$249**               | This case, every round, until Amazon decides (today's Pass)                                                                                                            |
| **Account Pass** | **$329**               | This case **plus every new Amazon notice on the same seller account for 12 months**: a listing complaint, an IP claim, a new verification request, a second suspension |

- This is Hormozi's **Buy X Get Y Free** (make the free part bigger than the paid part) shown as a **magnetic middle** ($80 more for a year). Most buyers should take it; that lifts average cash per buyer and, more important, opens the renewal in §6.5. For an agency the same 12 months of cover would be $1,000 for a single ASIN reinstatement (My Amazon Guy, V).
- **Needs code:** licence scoped to a seller account (the store name and business details the seller confirms), and a fair-use line in the Terms so one Pass is not run across many clients' accounts. Medium build.
- **Named bonuses (no new code; these exist):** the evidence pack, the deadline clock and reminders, Amazon's reply read and applied, the invoice-against-sales check, "what changed since last time", the root-cause coach. Each gets a name and a one-line "this answers…" (Hormozi: every bonus answers an objection; tools beat training).
- **Guarantee stack, named:** an unconditional 14-day money back (already true) **plus** "Every round included until Amazon decides" (already true). A name such as "The Every-Round Promise". The law allows the words "money-back guarantee" for the 14-day refund because it is a full refund on request (16 CFR 239.3); it must never sit beside a reinstatement claim.
- **Anchor:** a true, dated market strip above the prices: "Freelance writers $50–$600. Agencies $795–$2,500 per case. AppealDeck $249, every round." Hormozi's own 5–10× premium would be a done-for-you human service, which Paddle bars, so the honest anchor is the market.
- **MAGIC name check:** "Account Pass: 12 months of every Amazon notice, answered from your own facts" (Avatar implied, Goal, Interval, Container).

### 6.3 Downsells: only after a no

1. **Pay later, zero risk:** turn on Paddle's issuer and PayPal Pay Later instalments where offered (Paddle is paid in full). Say "Pay in parts where your card or PayPal offers it." Check the Paddle dashboard setting (U).
2. **Document Check, $59:** up to five business documents read against the notice, the invoice sums and units check, no response writing. **The full $59 is credited if the seller upgrades to a Pass within 30 days** (Rollover: $249 is 4.2× the credit, inside Hormozi's ≥4× rule). This catches the seller who already has a writer, and it is the writer's client-side purchase.
3. **Test later, not now:** a "final sale" Case Pass at $199 with no refund (Hormozi's guarantee-removal downsell). Only for non-EU/UK buyers (EU/UK are blocked today anyway) and only with the waiver shown before payment.

Not recommended: a self-built 3-payment subscription. The buyer can cancel after the case is done and Paddle cannot force the rest.

### 6.4 Upsells at checkout (Paddle cannot one-click after a one-time sale)

- **Classic, at the moment the problem appears:** when the notice names related accounts, "Add each linked account: $99" on the same checkout.
- **Warranty upsell (hold until data):** "Refused-in-full cover, +$49: if Amazon sends a final refusal within 100 days and you completed every step, the Pass and the cover are refunded." Break-even is a qualifying refusal rate of 16% (49 ÷ 298). The real rate is unknown, so this waits for 30 outcome reports (§8 stage 4).

### 6.5 Continuity: only where the need repeats

- **Account Cover renewal:** in month 11 of an Account Pass, "Keep every new notice covered for another year: $99/year" (or $12/month). Hormozi's continuity bonus: renew before the date and keep the full case history with it. A reinstated seller has been suspended once; the next notice is likelier for them than for anyone.
- **Case Pass buyers, after the outcome:** a one-time rollover, "Upgrade to Account Cover for $99 and your next 12 months are covered", offered within 30 days of the outcome report.
- **Not** a seller monthly plan at the front door (Hormozi; and the 21 Sep finding that a seller in crisis does not sign up monthly).
- Build the US ROSCA disclosures and, before January 2027, the UK reminders and online cancel into any renewal.

## 7. The professional ladder: where the monthly money is

Appeal writers and VA agencies have the repeating need Hormozi says continuity is for. No per-seat tool for professional appeal writers was found (U), so the slot is open.

| Plan   | Price          | For                                                                                |
| ------ | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Agency | **$399/month** | Unlimited cases, 5 seats, client-named exports and packs (shown first: the anchor) |
| Studio | **$149/month** | 25 active cases, client-named exports                                              |
| Solo   | **$49/month**  | 5 active cases                                                                     |
| Yearly | 10 × monthly   | Two months free (continuity discount)                                              |

- **Attraction:** a $1 first month (Paddle paid trial; Hormozi's "trial with penalty" for recurring offers: $1, not free). Or Win Your Money Back: "Run three client cases in your first month and the month is credited."
- **Comparison:** AppealsPro $79.99 / $199, SellerForge agency $4,990/yr for 10 accounts (V). A writer earns $50–$300 per POA; Solo at $49 costs about $10 a case.
- **Software only.** AppealDeck never sells the writer's service; the writer bills his client directly. That keeps Paddle's human-services rule intact.
- **Needs code:** seats, case limits per plan, client name on exports (G-60 already added a case label). Larger build.

### 7.1 Lead getters: the acquisition engine

- **Affiliate:** a writer or VA who sends a seller earns **30% of the Pass** ($75 on $249), paid monthly. AppealDeck keeps about $161; 30-day profit is 2.16× the cost of the customer, so every referred sale is level 3.
- **Two-sided:** the seller gets $25 off with the partner's code (Hormozi: rewards can go to one side or both).
- **Payout:** Paddle has no built-in affiliate payouts. Pay by Payoneer by hand for the first months; a tracking tool that supports Paddle later (U).
- **Customer referral:** "Know another seller? They get $25 off, you get $25 back."

## 8. Rollout: one stage at a time

Hormozi: perfect one offer, judge in quarters, raise when it works.

| Stage | Starts when                          | What goes live                                                                                                                                             | Measured by                                                                               |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Founder approves                     | Founding price ($149, first 25), named bonuses and guarantee stack, market strip, $25 outcome-report rebate, one free document check, the 30% partner code | Passes per 50 cases started; refund rate; 30-day cash per buyer; outcome reports received |
| 2     | 25 paid cases                        | Price to $249; Account Pass $329; pay-later; linked-account add-on                                                                                         | Share choosing Account Pass (target over half); revenue per 50 started                    |
| 3     | 3 writers or VAs ask to pay          | Solo / Studio / Agency plans, $1 first month                                                                                                               | Paying seats; month-2 retention                                                           |
| 4     | 50 paid cases and 30 outcome reports | Document Check $59 with rollover; price step to $299 then $349 while revenue per 50 started rises; refused-in-full cover if the refusal rate allows        | Revenue per 50 started; cover claims vs income                                            |
| 5     | First Account Passes reach month 11  | Account Cover renewal $99/year                                                                                                                             | Renewal rate                                                                              |

**The price rule (stage 2 onward):** after each step up, compare revenue per 50 cases started at the old and new price. Keep the rise while it goes up; step back once when it falls. Hormozi's "until the extra yeses no longer make up for the lost sales", made countable.

**Illustration, not a forecast (E):** 100 buyers at stage 2, 60% take the Account Pass and 40% the Case Pass: average cash $297 gross, about $281 net, against $236 today (+19%) before any renewal or professional plan.

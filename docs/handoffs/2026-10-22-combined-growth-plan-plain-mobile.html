What I corrected after reading the code

  • The $49 Document Check is struckThe document checks already run free on the device. We would be selling something free, and the AI reading it could sell cannot run until Gemini billing is on.
  • "One free check" is struckUnlimited free checks are the best thing in the free tier, and they cost us nothing.
  • The $25 rebate for reporting an outcome is struckOutcome sharing is anonymous on purpose, so we cannot know who to pay.
  • The founding price waitsIts bonus is the Account Pass, which does not exist yet.
  • Partner pay is 30 percent of what the buyer pays$67.20 on a $224 sale. Of the full $249 it would fall below the 2-times rule.

What waits, and the number that releases it

  • Switching on the Account PassYour choice, when you want it. The plan said after 25 paid cases; the code no longer has to wait.
  • Writer packs and a Pro planAfter three writers have run a real case. No price is shown until then.

Each needs a new type of licence and a database change, on a payment path that has never taken a real payment. The plan has the exact build steps ready.

Account history: reviewed direction

6 Nov 2026 · independent research and code review · documentation updated · product unbuilt

The direction is useful, but the original plan needed corrections. History can change the evidence and response route; no dataset shows how much it explains different outcomes.

Read the corrected master and source register. The original report and five research notes are historical supporting material.

Corrections that matter

  • Document allegation: preserve the original and Amazon's exact words. Flag it for review and respect the professional-review boundary. Do not permanently blacklist evidence needed for a supported dispute.
  • Admission: Amazon's PDF says a violation may still count after a successful appeal. Choosing “dispute” here does not change Amazon's record.
  • Deadlines: the three-day AHR process has scope and exceptions. Use the applicable notice and date; never start a universal clock from “critical”.
  • Invoices: distinguish issue-date eligibility from sales coverage. Match each cited product and include inventory where required; pooled invoice totals do not prove coverage.
  • Background: a yes answer does not automatically belong in the letter. Ask relevant questions, preserve unknowns, and let the seller review inclusions.
  • Privacy: new history fields would not automatically stay local. Explicit server/model payload controls must be designed and tested.

Stronger Amazon sources found

  • Staff multiple-account guidance: prior approval no longer required under stated legitimate-need/good-standing conditions; verify marketplace terms.
  • Seller Challenge announcement: eligible AHA listing reviews, three per 180 days, failed challenges replenish after six months; 48 hours is an aim.
  • Funds update: 60-day wait before requesting review; no automatic payout or deadline to apply within 60 days.

Recommended order

  1. Define sourced, scoped rules and evidence requirements.
  2. Connect existing submissions, attachments and replies; improve product-level invoice coverage.
  3. Add targeted repeat, document and account-history questions. Branch owned versus disputed related accounts immediately.
  4. Add cautious consistency checks and evaluate complete case journeys, including mistaken allegations and legitimate repeat submissions.

Keep history improvements ahead of further writing embellishments. The entire questionnaire is not established as a launch prerequisite. R-18 still awaits the founder's implementation selection.

What remains uncertain

Current target-market policy and acknowledgment flows need live verification. The AHR PDF is a Japan-hosted 2025 copy; invoice help text was retrieved through indexing, while direct opening returned the login shell. Practitioner experience, staff posts and policy text are different evidence types.

AppealDeck's inspected code has no outcome-training pipeline. Underlying model pretraining and live outcome counts were not verified. No success probability, automatic admission or guaranteed reinstatement is justified.

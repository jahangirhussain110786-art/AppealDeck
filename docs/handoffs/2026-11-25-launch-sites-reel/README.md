## What this means for AppealDeck (my notes, not the reel's)

- **Who you will get:** builders and founders, not suspended Amazon sellers. They are good **free testers** of how the site works (speed, clarity, bugs, design). They are **not** buyers, and they have no real Amazon notice.
- **Give them something to try without signing up:** the free decoder on `/decode` with the one-tap sample notice. Ask them to try it and say what was confusing.
- **Do not ask strangers to paste a real notice.** Point them to the sample and to `/how-we-test` (the 13 test notices).
- **The strongest one-line pitch for this audience:** "AppealDeck never logs in to Amazon or touches the seller's account; it reads the notice and checks your documents against it." Never claim results, odds or a "guarantee" (D6).
- **Backlinks (Uneed, Product Hunt, Indie Hackers)** also help search ranking for the `/guides` pages.
- **Before posting on Hacker News:** the GitHub repo is public (F-14). HN readers often look at the code. Decide F-14 first.
- **Real sellers are elsewhere:** the seller groups and the writer/VA-agency channel in the 12 Oct research (`2026-10-12-community-and-reach-research.md`, R-13) are still where buyers are. Use these five sites for testing and links, not sales.

## Suggested order (smallest risk first)

1. Uneed and Indie Hackers: quiet, friendly, a backlink.
2. r/SideProject: read the sub's rules on self-promotion first.
3. Product Hunt: needs a prepared page (pictures, short text); weekend if you want to rank.
4. Hacker News "Show HN": last, once the site has had a few rounds of fixes and F-14 is decided.

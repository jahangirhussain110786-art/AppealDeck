To do
Amazon asked for this. Your notice says:

"Include supporting documents, such as supplier invoices for the ASINs listed above."

Why: it proves you bought real stock from a real supplier. Amazon may phone the supplier to check.

What a good invoice shows
  • The supplier's name, address and phone number
  • Your business name and address, the same as on your seller account
  • A date in the last 12 months
  • The products (ASINs) and how many you bought, at least as many as you sold

Amazon will not accept: quotes, pro-forma invoices, order screenshots, shop receipts, spreadsheets you made, or edited PDFs.

What changes

  1. 4 tabs become 5 steps. Overview, Evidence, Response and History are app words. "Gather your documents" and "Send it to Amazon" are what the seller is actually doing.
  2. Any order. The steps are links, always visible at the top. One is marked "Start here", but nothing is locked.
  3. One thing open at a time. Each document opens on its own. Rarely used choices ("I can't get it", "This doesn't apply to me") move into one line of links underneath.
  4. One side card instead of four. Your deadline and where your files are. The timeline moves into step 5, and the privacy details into one link.
  5. Every question has an example of a good answer.

The writing rules (these become the rules for all app text)

  1. Short sentences. One idea each.
  2. Say "you" and "we". Start buttons with a verb: "Add", "Save", "Send".
  3. Use the seller's words, not ours: "documents", not "records" or "evidence"; "letter", not "response facts".
  4. Explain an Amazon word once, the first time: "a Plan of Action: a letter that says…"
  5. Every question gets an example answer.
  6. Say where to click in Seller Central, step by step.
  7. Never hide the honest parts. They get shorter, not removed.

What stays, on purpose

  • "Nothing is sent to Amazon. You send it yourself."
  • "We added this" on any document the notice doesn't name.
  • "We can't check this" beside the "finished" tick.
  • "Nobody can promise Amazon will say yes."
  • Your files stay on this computer, and "Check it for me" says what it sends.
  • Every feature that exists today. Nothing is removed. Some things are only folded away.

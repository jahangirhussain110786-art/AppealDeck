AM-31 (`2791358`) gave the whole app the v5 look: tokens, fonts, navy bands, pill buttons, sidebar, page headers. It did **not** rebuild each page's body. That list was worked through the same day in two batches: `84210f2` (shell, case page, dashboard) and the commit that follows it (decode result, pricing, support, home, privacy, vault, sign-in wording). Every page was screenshot-compared with its prototype at 1440 px; the signed-in pages were captured from a production build with the dev account.

## Deliberate differences (keep)

- **Tabs on the case page.** The prototype has none; Overview / Evidence / Response / History stay (e2e and deep links rely on them), drawn as an underlined row.
- **Honest-expectations card on /pricing.** The prototype's four lines alone would have removed D6's card; the card stays, in a row layout, before the consent box (`faq.spec.ts` pins the order).
- **"Only on this device" is hedged everywhere.** A seller who makes an encrypted cloud backup has a second copy (privacy policy §1, §5), so the prototype's "only copy" / "we have none" lines say "unless you choose an encrypted backup". The earlier data-flow diagram said "The only copy." and was corrected too.
- **No per-case "Appeal Pass active" in the sidebar.** A Pass belongs to one case; the sidebar says signed in or guest, and Pass status stays on the case and Billing.
- **No "Last check" column in the vault.** Checks are stored with the case, not the file list; showing one there would need a new join and could show a check against a different case.
- **Home hero and plan copy** keep the 25 Sep SEO wording, not the prototype's placeholder lines.
- The logo, the announcement bar, the full legal texts, the EU consent order, the operator paragraph's legal name, and every e2e-asserted label.
- Prototype sample data (Northline Trading, inv_0412, dates) is never rendered; real pages show the seller's own case.

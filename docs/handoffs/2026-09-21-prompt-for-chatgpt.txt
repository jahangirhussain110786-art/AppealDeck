Amazon has four different response types (appeal / plan of action / acknowledge / provide documents) and picking the wrong one is the #1 reason appeals die. That one decision is the whole product, and no page makes it.
Nothing reads the seller's files. An invoice sits in the vault as bytes. The system can't see the supplier's phone number is missing on page 1, so it can't say "here's what's wrong and here's the letter to fix it."
Nothing speaks first. A reminder date is stored and never delivered. An agency calls you; ours waits to be opened.
Two products live inside one app — the old step-by-step interview and the new case workspace, with two composers and two home screens. Sellers meet the seams.

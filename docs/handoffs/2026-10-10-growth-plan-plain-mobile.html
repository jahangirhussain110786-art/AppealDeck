Do now: 65 ideas

Three batches, about 9 days of work in total. Do them in this order.

Batch 1: fix what is wrong today (about 3 days)

These are mistakes a visitor can see right now. They are worth the most for the least work.

  • Stop copying stray "##" marks into Amazon's boxWhen a paying seller presses Copy on the finished response, the text includes "## Root Cause" and similar marks. Amazon's box would show those symbols.
  • Make the letter and the document pack agree on file namesThe letter calls a file one name. The download pack renames the same file. The person at Amazon sees something different from what the letter says.
  • Say the truth when a notice gives no deadlineToday the page says the deadline is "unclear" or "a window", when the notice says neither. Replace it with: no deadline is written, check the Appeal button in your Amazon account, and if a date is shown there, that date wins.
  • Fix the "Not stored" line next to the paste boxIt is no longer true. We keep a short fingerprint of the notice for 7 days (never the text itself). Say that plainly and match the privacy page word for word.
  • Show the $249 price where the seller decidesRight now the seller meets a sign-in button, with the price missing. Add one line: "Then $249 once for this case. Everything above stays free."
  • Rename the button "Open case workspace"No seller says "workspace". Use "Start my case, free". Some of our automatic tests look for the old name, so they change in the same step.
  • Tidy the pricing note on phonesIt sits as a three-line grey box above the heading. Make it one calm sentence.
  • Fix the product-safety adviceThe product tells a seller to upload a test report. For toys, supplements, batteries and some others, Amazon now takes the result straight from an approved lab, and a seller's own upload is no longer accepted. This is the one line of advice that could cost a seller their case. You said yes on 12 Oct

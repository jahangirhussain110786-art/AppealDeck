# AppealDeck: the master launch report (12 Oct 2026)

**What this is.** One compiled, current, complete report built from every research, audit and register file in this repository, so the founder can edit it or say GO. It replaces nothing; it points at the files that hold the evidence. Written 12 Oct 2026 at commit `49b13f4` and after, from the 10 Oct success assessment and growth register, the 12 Oct community, disclosure and indirect-competitor research, and a read-only inventory of every open item in `docs/`.

**How to read it.** Every number carries a tag from its source: **V** verified against a page or file line, **E** judgement, **U** unverified. IDs are the source files' own: G-xx growth register, X-xx community research, S-xx stay-and-pay, K-xx indirect competitors, A1 to A9 placement plan, P-xx parked, F-xx founder-only, R-xx review list, INV-xx the inventory, 0.x and 1.x the assessment's plan.

**The GO rule.** Saying "GO on the master report" releases every item in section 5.2 (the code and document batches), in that order, as batches with the usual gates. It does **not** release a parked item (section 5.5): each is built only on its exact `EXECUTE PARKED P-xx <name>` phrase, as the founder ruled on 8 Oct. It does not answer a founder decision (section 6): each is answered by its number, and the defaults listed there apply if the founder says nothing. Founder-only items (section 5.1) are the founder's and no GO starts them. Holds (section 5.4) wait for their triggers.

## 0. The short answer

- **The product is real and unusually complete for a solo build.** Thirty-three seller-facing capabilities are live and tested; ten more are built and wait only on a founder configuration step (section 1).
- **Whether it pays is decided by three things, none of them code:** a card must be chargeable (Paddle is the only rail for Pakistan; no plan C exists), someone must be sent to the site (the only fast door is appeal writers and VAs the founder reaches himself), and a stranger must pay $249 once for software. Odds of a first paid Pass within 60 days of a real launch: 25 to 40%, rising to 40 to 55% if the founder brings 10 to 20 writers or VAs to live cases (E, assessment §1).
- **What makes a visitor stay and pay is now specified and checked against evidence** (section 4): the three moments that decide it (the first minute, the gate, the first paid round), a treasure test the product scores 2 of 5 and 2 of 5 on today and 5 of 5 after the batches, and twenty S-items of which fourteen are already rows in the growth plan.
- **The indirect competitors are mostly a channel, not a threat** (section 3.2). Dastan is a human agency registered on 2 Oct 2025 that sells "20% upfront, 80% after reinstatement" through Fiverr and WhatsApp; ReKommerce sells a $295 non-refundable template pack; Ecommerce Success Pakistan sells $299 to $1,400 packages with a PKR 10,000 fee. For the seller's $249 they compete; for the writer channel the VA agencies and individual writers, who say in writing they cannot undo a suspension, are the door.
- **The work, in four layers** (section 5): decisions that cost nothing and unblock copy; the founder's configuration steps in dependency order; the code and document batches (about ten working days of AI sessions); then outreach. Nothing adds product capability; the 10 Oct rule (no new capability before 50 cases) holds.
- **Four things found this week that change the plan:** Paddle's seller-policy guidance says a refund window under 14 days fails review (the site promises 7; F-15); the checkout has no "I have read the Terms" control; the GitHub repository is public (F-14); and the sign-out question (F-12) is already closed in code.

## 4. The treasure test: what makes a seller stay and pay

From the 12 Oct stay-and-pay research (`2026-10-12-indirect-competitors-and-retention-research.md` §4), built on reviews and forum threads fetched the same day.

**What the market praises and punishes (V, Trustpilot, Feefo, forums).** The 5-star reviews of the human firms praise a **named person** ("Chris was present, quick to reply"; "I would give Holly 100 stars"), being **kept informed** ("he was always checking in, following up"; "kept me informed every step of the way" over 5.5 months), **speed**, **persistence across rounds** ("wrote no fewer than 5 appeals and the last one worked"; "took 6 months, never gave up"), **honesty about chances** ("made no promises") and a **clear plan with the documents explained**. Price is almost never mentioned. The 1-star reviews are **silence** ("I stopped hearing from them altogether for nearly two weeks"), **templates** ("all it was template emails"; "For $300+ you get a generic 4-5 sentences"), **ChatGPT** ("clearly AI-generated (literally ChatGPT prompts)"), **paid and nothing** ("No appeals were ever filed"; "$1,800 up front and do absolutely nothing"), and **refund trouble** ("only half returned"). In the UK Seller Forums thread "Should I pay £3,200?", the top reply (160 upvotes) says skip the paid service, "the web is full of scammers"; the only recommendation is a named person with a count.

**What this means for AppealDeck (E).** Every complaint cluster is something the product already refuses by design (no template: the fact lock; cannot ghost: the case always says what is next; no login: AM-27; the refund is stated). But two of them are invisible today (the fact lock has no visible stamp, G-35; nothing shows a guest the paid artefacts) and one is not yet real (no named person on the page, no mailbox: G-47, F-05).

**The three moments that decide "stay and pay", and where the product falls short today (V, the 10 Oct walk; no `src` change since):**

1. **The first minute:** "Use a sample" needs a second tap (G-77); the deadline tile says "ambiguous" when nothing is written (G-87); the money question goes unanswered (G-03); the scam check does not say what it looked for (G-48); the result is thirteen phone screens; the first button says "Open case workspace" (G-88).
2. **The gate:** the big button says sign in, the small text says a Pass, no price on the screen (G-30), and nothing of the paid artefact is visible except the pricing-page sample POA. The pack index and the document-check report, the two things no rival has and a professional judges the price by, are never shown to a guest (**S-01**, new).
3. **The first paid round:** the prepared response shows and copies "## Root Cause" (G-28); the letter names a file the pack renamed (G-29); nothing after activation says what the buyer now owns, and the purchase email omits "every round, no expiry" (**S-05**, new; G-39); the pack sits under the activity log (G-62); the fact lock is described, not shown (G-35); Amazon's silence after sending is unexplained (G-69); nobody is named (F-05).

## 7. What a GO means, and how it runs

1. **Before the first AI batch:** the founder answers the layer-1 decisions he wants answered now (section 6 A; the rest default as recommended) and writes his one-line sentence (G-47) when he is ready; nothing in a batch waits on it except the trust-band line itself.
2. **Order:** batch A → the disclosure batch → batch B → batch C → the X items → the documents (section 5.2). About ten to twelve working days of AI sessions, six commits.
3. **Each batch:** one commit, all gates green, a browser check at 390 and 1440 px, pushed, CI checked, the registers updated in the same commit, the evidence written into a companion log (`docs/handoffs/2026-10-12-master-launch-log.md`, opened with the first batch).
4. **In parallel, the founder's layer 2** in the order of section 5.1: domain → mailboxes → Supabase SMTP and OAuth → secrets and Plausible → Pro plans → the Paddle copy pass → Paddle approval with the refund window decided → Gemini billing and R-1 → backup drill → uptime monitor → the live test purchase → the 16 post-deploy checks.
5. **Launch** is the day the live test purchase succeeds. From then, section 8, and layer 4 begins.
6. **What a GO does not do:** release any P-xx item; change any locked decision (D1 to D10, AM-27, AM-28); build any "hold" item before its trigger; add any product capability before 50 cases; publish any claim the lint gate bans.

## 8. After launch: the routine

- **Daily (one fixed hour):** read the mailboxes; answer inside two business days with the ten canned answers and the "will I win?" script (community research §6); refunds the same day; log every contact (X-05).
- **Weekly (2 to 3 hours):** the notice clinic in the existing groups (X-01); writers and VAs in Lahore and Karachi (the K-02 list, the K-04 scripts, the Cal.com link, the one-tap sample and the "How we test" page; never a success claim; D5 agreement before any access); the numbers sheet.
- **Monthly:** the fresh-wording routine on 10 new notices or replies (R-8; next due about 9 Nov 2026) with the miss count published on "How we test"; Gemini deprecations and price page; Seller Assistant release notes; the Gemini prepay top-up; dependency updates; one problem page once the real domain exists.
- **Quarterly:** the 90-day policy brief re-check (next about 5 Jan 2027); refund and chargeback review; the outcomes ledger once it has a denominator (P-13); re-verify the competitor price anchors (now including the Pakistani points).
- **Kill rules (assessment §7):** Paddle refused twice with no fixable reason, or Google billing impossible after support and a reseller → the free decoder stays up and the business is a different business; 50 cases started and under 1 Pass → the price-shape tests in the pre-agreed order, no features; two missed monthly routines in a row → the key-person signal.

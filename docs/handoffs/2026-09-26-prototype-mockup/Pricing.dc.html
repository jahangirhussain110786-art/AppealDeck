Questions sellers ask before paying

Does one Pass cover a second response if Amazon refuses the first?
Yes. A Pass covers one case for as long as that case runs: every revision, every round of reply, no extra charge and no expiry.
Do you submit the appeal for me?
No, and we never log in to your account. Amazon's Agent Policy binds you, the seller, so a tool that touched Seller Central would put the account you are trying to recover at risk.
What happens to my invoices?
They are stored encrypted on your device. With a Pass, a document you choose is read on our server to check it against the notice, and is not kept afterwards.
Is there a case you will not take?
Forged documents, fraud and child-safety matters are routed to professional help and never sold a Pass. Everything else gets a workspace, including notice types other tools refuse.
How do I get a refund?
Email support within 7 days of purchase. No reason needed. Refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method.

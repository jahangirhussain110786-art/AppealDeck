Hello,

Your Amazon seller account has been deactivated in accordance with section 3 of Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement. We took this measure because we have concerns about the authenticity of the items listed at the end of this email. Listings for these items have been removed.

To reactivate your account, please send us copies of invoices or receipts from your supplier issued in the last 365 days. These should reflect your sales volume during that time and include the supplier's contact information. Also send a plan that explains the root cause of the issue and the steps you have taken to prevent it from happening again.

You have 72 hours to respond. If we do not receive the requested information, your account will remain deactivated and funds will be held in your account while we review.

You can see your account's status and submit this information in the Account Health section of Seller Central.

# What appeal firms, consultants and attorneys ask a seller before writing a POA (intake practice)

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] read on the firm's own published page (URL given); [P] practitioner statement in an article, podcast or interview; [U] unverified (forum anecdote, search snippet only, or a page that could not be opened). Secondary summaries from a fetch tool were read for each [V] page; quotes are short phrases only. No success rates are reported, and none should be taken from these pages: several are marketing.

Coverage note for the report writer: firms almost never publish a full intake form. Most "free case review" pages collect contact details and the notice, and the real questions happen on a call or by email after sign-up. The richest published lists came from ASA Compliance Group (amazonsellersappeal.com), AMZ Sellers Attorney (amazonsellers.attorney), and Amazon Sellers Lawyer (CJ Rosenbaum). ecommerceChris, eGrowth Partners, Riverbend and Seller Candy publish no field-level intake list that could be found.

## 1. Published intake forms and "what we need" pages

### Takeaway
The few published intake forms are short (contact details, the notice pasted or attached, revenue, free text). The documented "what we need" lists live on firm FAQ and guide pages, and they are organised by the type of case, not as one fixed questionnaire.

### Cited Findings
- [V] A Tally form titled "Get your case reviewed" (no firm named on the page; it surfaced in a search for ecommerceChris, but the link to that firm is NOT confirmed) asks: full name (required), email (required), WhatsApp number, approximate monthly revenue, how did you find us, "Paste your suspension notice or the message Amazon sent", an attachment (screenshot, PDF, .eml, .doc, up to 10 MB), and "Anything else you'd like us to know about your case?". It promises "no charge to review, no obligation." — [Tally form](https://tally.so/r/Y5voaW)
- [U] A search-result summary of the same or a similar form said it also asks whether the seller has opened another Amazon account since the suspension; the fetched form did not show that field. — [Tally form](https://tally.so/r/Y5voaW)
- [V] ASA Compliance Group (amazonsellersappeal.com; metrics "last verified" 1 Nov 2026) says a free review starts from three things: Amazon's relevant message, the case history, and previous submissions. Its fuller list is: the affected account or ASINs, previous submissions and Amazon's replies, and "a short timeline". It "may request essential missing records before we can assess the appropriate next step." — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] AMZ Sellers Attorney (buyer's guide, published 15 May 2026, updated 9 Oct 2026) lists what an appeal needs: the exact suspension notice and case number; supplier invoices that "match sold quantities"; tracking and delivery confirmations for performance cases; written procedures for sourcing, shipping and customer service; brand authorization letters or certificates of authenticity; Account Health dashboard screenshots; and any prior communications with Amazon. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Riverbend Consulting's 3P appeal page lists no documents. It tells the seller to "carefully examine" the notice and write down "what happened and how it happened" before contacting Amazon, and says Riverbend itself runs reports and "asks questions" on a follow-up call. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [U] For vendor (1P) accounts, Riverbend says it reviews all notifications and case-log messages the seller provides (search snippet). — [Riverbend vendor page](https://riverbendconsulting.com/vendor-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [U] FreeUp's suspension appeal service reportedly asks for the seller name and store ID, the full suspension letter, and any receipts, invoices and supplier contact information Amazon requested (search snippet only; the fetched page showed no such list). — [FreeUp](https://freeup.net/suspension-appeal-service)
- [U] Fiverr/Upwork gig listings (pages returned 403 to the fetch tool; only search snippets seen) commonly request the suspension notification email, supplier invoices, payment receipts and tracking numbers, a brief account history, and prior appeals. One gig said it may request access to the seller's account plus identity and payment documents. — [Fiverr example](https://fiverr.com/umer_khalid144/reinstate-your-deactivated-amazon-account); [Fiverr example](https://fiverr.com/mohammadfaiz01/amazon-account-health-management-customer-support-reim); [Upwork example](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/consulting-hr-reinstate-amazon-account-for-section-3-inauthentic-or-related-link-2075609887057782912)

### Inferences
- The published entry point is "paste the notice + tell us anything else". The diagnostic questions are asked after intake, so a tool that asks them up front is doing what firms do on the call.
- Free text and a monthly revenue figure are the only account-level fields on the one form found; revenue is likely used for pricing and triage rather than for the POA.

### Gaps
- No published field-level intake form was found for ecommerceChris, eGrowth Partners, Riverbend, Seller Candy (its suspension page returned 404), Jeff Coleman / Coleman Legal, SellerPlex, AppealsPro, or any Pakistani/Indian agency. Not checked this round: Coleman Legal, AppealsPro, SellerPlex, Pakistani agencies.
- Fiverr/Upwork "requirements" sections could not be read directly (403).

## 2. Findings grouped by category

### Takeaway
Consensus items across firms: the full Amazon notice, every prior appeal and Amazon's replies, and the case-specific documents (invoices for authenticity, test reports for compliance, ownership records for IP). Account-level background (business model, age, volume, other accounts, who has access) is mentioned mainly in related-account guidance and by inference, not as a published form field.

### Cited Findings

(a) About the account
- [V] Affected account or ASINs, plus "a short timeline". — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Related-account cases: Seller ID and account details; whether the seller owns another account; who or what may have caused the link (e.g. a shared IP or a VA's login); IP logs and records of access points; proof of separate business structures; communications with third-party services that may have caused the issue. Stated reason: Amazon links accounts through shared IPs, devices, bank accounts, cards, addresses, tax details, family or partners, VA/employee access, and third-party services logging into several accounts. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, related accounts](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/fix-amazon-related-account-suspension/) (no date; © 2026)
- [V] Approximate monthly revenue (one form). — [Tally form](https://tally.so/r/Y5voaW)
- [V] For business acquisitions: account history, performance and policy records. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [U] Brief overview of account history (gig snippets). — see Fiverr links above.
- No source found that publishes a field for account age, business model (wholesale / private label / arbitrage / dropship), FBA vs FBM, or Account Health Rating as a number. Business model is implied by what documents are asked for (brand authorization and distributor invoices imply resale).

(b) About the notice
- [V] "Start with Amazon's relevant message"; reason: to identify the issue and the response route. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] The exact notice and the case number. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Paste the notice or attach it (.eml accepted). — [Tally form](https://tally.so/r/Y5voaW)
- [V] The POA should respond directly to the concerns in Amazon's notice. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/) (CJ Rosenbaum; no date; cites 2025 cases)
- [P] A seller guide advises reading the Performance Notification side by side with the Account Health dashboard, and reviewing the affected marketplace and account status. — [Olifant Digital](https://olifantdigital.com/blog/amazon-account-suspended); [StoreStride](https://www.storestride.com/blog/amazon-account-suspension-appeal) (search snippets; which sentence came from which page not confirmed)

(c) About prior appeals
- [V] Previous submissions and Amazon's replies, as part of the free review. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Any prior communications with Amazon. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Stated reason: an escalated POA must analyse why Amazon rejected the earlier one, and "each denial" makes later appeals harder. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/)
- [V] Riverbend warns that minor revisions to the same appeal submitted too quickly "can get your account blocked or banned", which is why it wants to see what was already sent. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)

(d) Documents (by case type)
- [V] Authenticity: "original, unaltered invoices" linking inventory to supplier and product identifiers, within the date range Amazon specifies. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Authenticity: manufacturer or authorized-distributor invoices, "as complete a paper trail from brand to your business as possible"; invoice quantities matched to sales history; supplier and manufacturer correspondence; product images including UPCs; brand-owner authorization letters; order confirmations compared against total sales (case example). — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/)
- [V] Invoice rules from AMZ Sellers Attorney (post dated 18 Aug 2020, content retitled "Amazon Invoice Requirements 2025"): dated within 365 days; unique invoice number and final status (no pro-forma, PO or quote); supplier name, street address, phone, website and email; the seller's legal entity and address matching Seller Central; brand/model/GTIN matching the ASIN; quantities shown (prices may be redacted, but blurring or digital alteration is "flagged"); proof of payment; a VAT/tax notation; PDF or clear image, not editable files; submit three recent invoices covering the flagged sales. The firm's claims about Amazon's checks (expanded "Document Authenticity Review" team in Q1 2025, supplier cross-checks) are its own and not sourced to Amazon. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney, invoices](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/what-invoices-amazon-expects-from-amazon-sellers)
- [V] IP cases: ownership records, a licence, or another basis for lawful use. Regulatory cases: test reports, certifications, labels, manufacturer records. Hazmat/battery: the manufacturer's safety data sheet or a battery/exemption sheet where Amazon requests it. Condition cases: order, inspection, returns and fulfillment records and photos. Review-manipulation: buyer messages, inserts, review-request templates, and records from any agency or software used. Verification (KYC): identity, business registration, bank statements, proof of address, beneficial-owner information. Reason stated per item: to match what Amazon's request names, "only where relevant". — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Performance cases: tracking and delivery confirmations. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [P] For inauthentic cases, one guide lists supplier invoices (last 365 days), a COA, and supplier business licences. — [SmartScout](https://www.smartscout.com/blog/amazon-appeal-process) (search snippet)
- [U] A seller-forum thread describes Amazon first asking for invoices for specific ASINs, then invoices from an earlier date plus a POA. — [Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d5f3de9499d78fd4a0734f840c069c7b) (search snippet)

(e) The seller's own explanation and changes made
- [V] Riverbend: write down "what happened and how it happened" and the steps being taken, before contacting Amazon. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [V] Rosenbaum: an explanation of sourcing practices, the company's compliance procedures, corrective actions already implemented, and (where relevant) a commitment to remove products and an explanation of any decision not to relist. Stated reasons: context for the supply-chain documents and showing the problem is addressed. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/)
- [V] Written procedures for sourcing, shipping and customer service. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] ASA: responses must rest on actual records; sellers should not invent causes, admissions or corrective actions. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)

(f) Anything else
- [V] Account Health dashboard screenshots. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Customer communications (where relevant). — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Riverbend says its assessment "goes far deeper than simply reading" the notice: it runs reports and searches for trouble areas. This implies account data (performance reports) is pulled, but the page does not say how or with what access. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [V] Rosenbaum says video interviews are "becoming standard for Section 3 cases" (preparation, not a document). — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/)
- No published source found that names Voice of the Customer, Customer Service Performance data, return reasons or negative feedback as intake items by name.

### Inferences
- Consensus (three or more independent sources): the full notice; prior appeals and Amazon's replies; invoices for authenticity cases with supplier details and quantities that match sales; the seller's written account of what happened and what changed.
- Two sources: Account Health screenshots / Amazon communications (AMZ Sellers Attorney, ASA); brand authorization letters (Rosenbaum, AMZ Sellers Attorney); written SOPs (Rosenbaum, AMZ Sellers Attorney).
- Single source: "short timeline" (ASA); case number (AMZ Sellers Attorney); review-manipulation records incl. agency/software records (ASA); IP logs and third-party-service communications for related accounts (Rosenbaum); WhatsApp and revenue (Tally form, unattributed); video interview prep (Rosenbaum).
- The case-type branching in ASA's list (authenticity / IP / regulatory / hazmat / condition / reviews / KYC) is the clearest published model of "which documents for which notice".

### Gaps
- No firm publishes questions on business model, account age, FBA/FBM, sales volume, AHR, or prior warnings as explicit fields. They likely come up on the intake call, but no source confirms the exact questions.
- Reddit/r/AmazonSeller threads describing what a consultant asked were not surfaced by search.

## 3. Seller Central login access and the Agent Policy

### Takeaway
None of the firm pages read asks for a Seller Central password. Legal-firm guidance says to use User Permissions instead of shared credentials; some low-cost gigs do ask for account access. Since 2025 Amazon has moved third-party access off secondary-user seats and onto the Solution Provider Portal, and the 4 Mar 2026 BSA update added the Agent Policy; no firm statement on how it affects appeal work was found.

### Cited Findings
- [V] Rosenbaum (related accounts): use Amazon's User Permissions instead of shared credentials, limit access to trusted employees, one account per device, password managers to prevent cross-account logins. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/fix-amazon-related-account-suspension/)
- [V] Riverbend, ASA and AMZ Sellers Attorney pages read say nothing about login access. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/); [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/); [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] ASA offers an NDA before sensitive records are shared. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [U] One Fiverr gig says it may request access to the seller's account plus identity and payment documents (search snippet). — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/umer_khalid144/reinstate-your-deactivated-amazon-account)
- [P] Newsletter (early 2025, relaying Vanessa Hung, CEO of Online Seller Solutions): secondary-user access to be limited to "sellers' direct employees"; agencies, freelancers and VAs must register through the Solution Provider Portal (SPP); deadline 31 Aug 2025; "Misclassification of users could affect seller accounts." Not sourced to an Amazon page. — [AMZ Agency Edge](https://amzagencyedge.kit.com/posts/secondary-user-access-overhaul-the-august-31st-deadline-free-ppc-tool-update)
- [V] Amazon's own forum announcement of the BSA update includes a new Agent Policy with requirements for AI usage and automated systems, and says Amazon may restrict access under it. — [Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/84e3f6b1-42f7-4cf3-a189-a5cc8d78d838) (search snippet of the official post)
- [P] Third-party commentary says the update expands the "agent" definition to AI tools and third-party agencies. — [Ecomclips](https://ecomclips.com/blog/amazon-bsa-agent-policy-2026-what-every-seller-must-do-before-march-4-to-protect-rankings-sales/) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Professional firms appear to work from what the seller sends (notice, exports, screenshots) rather than logging in, which matches the document lists above. Riverbend's "runs reports" may need access or seller-run exports; the page does not say which.

### Gaps
- No appeal firm statement on the Agent Policy's effect on its own practice was found.

## 4. What firms refuse or warn against

### Takeaway
Firms warn against altered invoices, "ungating" invoices and guarantees; explicit published lists of declined cases are rare.

### Cited Findings
- [V] AMZ Sellers Attorney: "any alteration triggers suspension"; red flags include edited PDF metadata, mismatched fonts, disconnected supplier phones, newly registered supplier websites; invoices from "ungating" or "wholesale list" services are treated as fraud. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney, invoices](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/what-invoices-amazon-expects-from-amazon-sellers)
- [V] ASA: will advise when reinstatement looks unlikely, may refer a case to another specialist, does not handle litigation or court filings, does not guarantee reinstatement, and will not invent causes, admissions or corrective actions. — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] AMZ Sellers Attorney warns that non-attorney consultancies "often advertise guarantees", flags per-revision and escalation fees and contingency cuts of recovered funds, and says "A provider that will not quote before reviewing your case is usually billing hourly." (Written by the founder of the first-listed firm.) — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Riverbend: "Don't react"; do not send anything to Amazon until the plan is written; do not submit multiple appeals too quickly. — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [U] A Fiverr listing says no ethical consultant can guarantee reinstatement (search snippet). — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/dianajayson/write-appeal-for-amazon-account-reinstatement)

### Gaps
- No firm page read publishes a refused-case list (e.g. fraud, forged documents, child safety). Rosenbaum's page does not address forged documents.

## 5. How firms structure the process

### Takeaway
Typical shape: free review from the notice → fee quoted in writing → intake call / document collection → root-cause diagnosis → POA drafting → submission and follow-ups/revisions → escalation (Amazon Legal, legal letter, arbitration) for law firms.

### Cited Findings
- [V] ASA: free review → identify issue and missing records → approach, scope and fee in writing → prepare response, revisions and follow-ups (a POA "where the issue calls for one"). — [ASA FAQ](https://amazonsellersappeal.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-faq/)
- [V] Riverbend: "Assess & Assist" (research, reports, questions, call with a consultant on root cause) → "Create & Communicate" (appeal letter + POA; further appeals and escalations "for one flat rate") → post-reinstatement advice. Standard appeal "Four business days or less"; rapid "as quickly as 1 business day". — [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/3p-account-suspension-appeal/)
- [V] AMZ Sellers Attorney: free consultation and "Live intake 24/7"; flat fees $1,500 (standard/Section 3) and $2,300 (IP/related account) "quoted before any work begins"; steps: review, root-cause diagnosis, evidence collection, POA drafting, submission, follow-up, escalation to Amazon Legal or AAA arbitration, post-reinstatement compliance review. No attorney-client relationship until a written engagement is signed. — [AMZ Sellers Attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/how-to-choose-the-best-amazon-appeal-service-in-2025)
- [V] Rosenbaum: first POA → escalated POA analysing the rejection → legal letter → pre-arbitration letter. — [Amazon Sellers Lawyer, inauthentic POA guide](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeals/amazon-inauthentic-suspension-appeal-plan-of-action-guide-going-into-2026/)
- [U] ecommerceChris: a single third-party review says costs were explained after the case review; Trustpilot lists support@ecommercechris.com as contact. — [webretailer review](https://webretailer.com/reviews/ecommercechris); [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.ecommercechris.com)
- [U] eGrowth Partners: a 2025 Trustpilot reviewer cites a contract requiring at least $2,500 before service. — [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.egrowthpartners.com)

### Gaps
- ecommerceChris, eGrowth Partners and Seller Candy process pages were not found or not readable; their intake content is unverified.

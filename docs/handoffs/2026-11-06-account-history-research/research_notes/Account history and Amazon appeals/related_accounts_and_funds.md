# Related / linked seller accounts and held funds, as they affect reinstatement

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Tags: **[V]** official Amazon text (URL given), **[P]** practitioner (name + URL), **[U]** forum anecdote or unverified. No success rates appear here. None were found from a reliable source, and the task forbids them.

**Method caveat:** Seller Central help pages (`/help/hub/reference/external/...`) render client-side and returned only navigation when fetched. The official multiple-accounts help page, the Funds Disbursement Eligibility Policy page and the Agent Policy page could therefore **not** be read directly. Their wording below comes from Amazon forum threads that reproduce it, and is tagged [U] or [V-reproduced] accordingly. The one official text read in full is the BSA PDF hosted on Amazon's own CDN.

## 1. Amazon's official multiple-accounts policy, the "legitimate business need" rule, and the related-account notice wording

### Takeaway
By default one Seller Central account is allowed per seller per region. A second account needs a "legitimate business need", its own bank account and its own email address, and (in the older wording) prior approval via Contact Us. The live help page could not be fetched, and practitioners disagree on whether prior approval is still required. Related-account notices say the account is "related to" another account, often naming only its first letters, and ask the seller to say whether they recognise it.

### Cited Findings
- [U, reproduces Amazon help text, old] "Operating and maintaining multiple Seller Central accounts is prohibited. If you have a legitimate business need for a second account, you can apply for an exception to this policy." The request goes through Contact Us ("Other account issues") and must explain the business need. — [Seller Forums thread quoting the help page](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cee006de62f974da4e03889377d4344e)
- [U, reproduces Amazon help text] "You must have a separate bank account for each Seller Central account. We will not approve multiple Seller Central accounts that use the same bank account within the same region." Accounts linked through Amazon Global Selling may share a bank account across regions. Each account needs a separate email address, and one version adds that the products sold must differ. — [same thread](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cee006de62f974da4e03889377d4344e); [Seller Forums (SCE), "Multiple Account Policy"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/79770b88aeee55780805d44a3217a258)
- [U] An Amazon community post words the rule as: multiple accounts allowed only if "you have a legitimate business need" and "all of your accounts are in good standing". It warns that if one account "violates an Amazon policy and is deactivated, this could affect the selling ability across all associated accounts". — [Seller Forums search result](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/forums/t/seller-with-multiple-accounts/448406?page=2)
- **Disagreement:** [P] BellaVix says that since April 2020 sellers can open multiple accounts without prior approval from Amazon. [P] AmazonSellersLawyer says that "as of 2025" Amazon permits multiple accounts without prior approval if two conditions are met. MyAmazonGuy and forum posters describe a request-and-approval route, and one poster warns that a second account without approval risks a suspension "that takes 1-3 months to resolve". — [BellaVix](https://www.bellavix.com/can-you-have-more-than-one-amazon-seller-account-heres-what-to-know/); [AmazonSellersLawyer](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/multiple-amazon-seller-accounts/); [MyAmazonGuy](https://myamazonguy.com/seller-central/seller-central-amazon-accounts/)
- [P] Sources also disagree on whether two accounts may share one tax ID or legal entity. The policy excerpts quoted above do not mention tax IDs. — [SellerApp 2026](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/multiple-amazon-seller-accounts/); [MyAmazonGuy](https://myamazonguy.com/seller-central/seller-central-amazon-accounts/)
- [U, Amazon notice as posted by a seller] Thread title: "You have been found to be related to an account". The appeal form offers the option "I recognise the other account but it does not belong to me" and asks the seller to "Provide us with supporting information to substantiate your claim." — [Seller Forums (SCE)](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/02f329bf-fe76-458e-ac70-af671b635e06)
- [U] Other quoted notice forms: "You have been found to be related to an account beginning with…" ([SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/162a74eeae6e2f0b4d94d9378e50609a)); "You have a separate account Fear0…pparel which was enforced for violating one of our policies" (reader comment, Nov 2021, [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/)). Related-account deactivations are commonly issued "in accordance with section 3 of Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement" (comment, Jan 2024, same page).
- [V] BSA Section 3 (US/NA text, Amazon CDN PDF, file links to a Jul 19 2022 translation, version otherwise unconfirmed) allows suspension or termination "immediately" if "(b) your account has been, or our controls identify that it may be used for deceptive or fraudulent, or illegal activity; (c) your use of the Services has harmed, or our controls identify that it might harm, other sellers, customers, or Amazon's legitimate interests". Amazon notifies the seller "indicating the reason and any options to appeal, **except where we have reason to believe that providing this information will hinder the investigation… or will enable you to circumvent our safeguards**." — [Amazon BSA PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/rainier/help./1.1_BSA_PDF_English.pdf)
- [V] Section 3 also covers termination for convenience with 30 days' notice, and a 7-day cure notice for material breach. — same PDF

### Inferences
- The circumvention carve-out in BSA §3 is the contractual reason related-account notices are vague and do not name the other account or the linking signal.
- A tool cannot treat "second account" as automatically wrongful. It must ask whether the seller had approval or a documented business need, whether each account had its own bank account and email, and whether the accounts sell different products.

### Gaps
- The current live wording of the Seller Central "Operating multiple accounts" help page, and whether prior approval is still required in November 2026, could not be fetched (the page is JS-rendered).
- No official Amazon list of the "significant points of information" was found. Amazon does not publish its linking signals.

## 2. What links accounts in practice

### Takeaway
Amazon confirms only that a linked account shares "at least one significant point of information". Practitioners list identity, financial, location, device/network, people and behavioural links, and give financial identifiers (bank account, tax ID) the most weight.

### Cited Findings
- [V-ish, Amazon staff reply on forum] "Your account does share at least one significant point of information with this other account." — Abella_Amazon, [Seller Forums (SCE)](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/02f329bf-fe76-458e-ac70-af671b635e06)
- [P] Traverse Legal (reviewed by Enrico Schaefer, 17 Jul 2026) lists: business and home addresses; phone numbers; tax information; bank accounts, deposit methods and credit cards; IP addresses, device history and browser activity; login behaviour; shared employees, service providers, inventory and operations; and behavioural signals (similar listing patterns, shared suppliers, overlapping inventory, matched catalogs, coordinated activity). It notes that no single fact always proves common control, and that a shared bank account or tax ID "carries more weight". — [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/amazon-linked-accounts/)
- [P] Dave Bryant (EcomCrew, updated 21 Mar 2024) lists: identical business addresses or names; shared wireless networks; logins from the same computer; giving API access to a company whose API credentials were revoked; and giving user permissions to someone who had a suspended seller account. He says internal documentation he saw had "well over twenty factors". This is his claim and is unverified. — [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/)
- [P] VA, employee or third-party agency in common is listed as a trigger. Sharing a main login, or a VA moving between accounts, creates IP/device/cookie overlaps. — [AmazonSellersLawyer, 2025 crackdown](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/related-accounts-amazon-suspension)
- [U] Shared phone number via a removal order ([SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/b11c0315e6a90c566fda9bcdeb49002e)). Family member's or former partner's account: forum replies advise that if the relationship "you once owned is over", you must explain it with proofs. — [Seller Forums results summary, multiple threads](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/162a74eeae6e2f0b4d94d9378e50609a)
- [P] Chris McCabe (ecommerceChris, former Amazonian) has described otherwise healthy accounts shut down over "a slight relation" to a suspended account. He says a new account opened to evade a suspension is likely closed for "relations to an account that may not be used to sell on our site". — [WebRetailer post by McCabe](https://www.webretailer.com/b/amazon-seller-suspensions); [Seller Performance Solutions podcast](https://rss.com/podcasts/seller-performance-solutions/1643944/)
- [P] Lesley Hensell (Riverbend co-founder) called linked accounts the biggest reason for suspension on Amazon, typically when a seller opens a new account after a past one was suspended. — [SyncSpider podcast](https://syncspider.com/podcast/how-to-get-your-suspended-amazon-account-reinstated-with-lesley-hensell-co-founder-at-riverbend-consulting/)

### Inferences
- The facts a tool must collect fall into seven groups: people (owner, directors, employees, VAs, agencies, family); identity documents and tax ID; bank accounts and cards; addresses (business, home, return, removal-order); phone and email; devices, networks and IPs (shared Wi-Fi, coworking space, VA machine); and third-party access (user permissions, API apps). Each should be recorded with dates, because a *past* link (former partner, ex-VA) is explained differently from a current one.

### Gaps
- No official Amazon source lists linking signals or ranks their weight. Every list above comes from practitioners.

## 3. Does the healthy account follow, and how must the appeal handle the other account?

### Takeaway
Being related is not itself the trigger. Being related to an account that is *currently deactivated* is. Amazon staff state the sequence: reinstate the related account first through its own appeal, then appeal the second account citing that reinstatement. Where the other account is not the seller's, the appeal must say what the shared fact is, why it exists, and document separation. Practitioners warn against denying facts Amazon can prove.

### Cited Findings
- [V-ish, Amazon staff] The related account must be reactivated first through its own appeal. The seller then screenshots its home page and appeals from the current account via "Submit additional information", selecting "I recognize the other account". — Abella_Amazon, [Seller Forums (SCE)](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/02f329bf-fe76-458e-ac70-af671b635e06)
- [P] "Simply being related to another account isn't resulting in suspensions. It's being related to another account that is currently suspended." — Dave Bryant, [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/). **Disagreement:** [P] AmazonSellersLawyer describes the trigger as a link to another account "whether active, deactivated, or in violation of policies". — [AmazonSellersLawyer](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/blog/suspension/how-to-resolve-related-account-suspension-on-amazon/)
- [P] Bryant's sequence: call Account Health and ask for the first three letters of the related account's email; check User Permissions History for revoked access, which may point to a service provider; if possible, get the related seller to reinstate their own account first; otherwise prove non-relation with utility bills, business licence and identity documents. — [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/)
- [P] Traverse Legal: "Amazon treats related accounts as an identity and control problem." Start with who owns the business and who controls the bank, inventory and decisions. "Sellers should not deny facts that Amazon can prove… A false denial can make the case worse." State the shared fact, why it exists and why it does not show common control, with a labelled evidence index (formation records, ownership ledgers, bank letters, leases, utility bills, 3PL or supplier agreements, payroll). Amazon wants to see that "proceeds do not flow to the same person or entity behind the related account." — [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/amazon-linked-accounts/)
- [U, reproduced Amazon warning via Traverse] "account issues can lead to the deactivation of related accounts until the issues are resolved." — [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/amazon-linked-accounts/)
- [P] McCabe has said Amazon's messaging "often mislead[s] sellers into filing ineffective appeals" on related accounts. — [Seller Performance Solutions podcast](https://podcasts.bcast.fm/e/2863k9qn)

**Why the same violation and the same POA can reinstate one account and not another**, as the sources explain it:
- [V-ish/P] The second account's appeal is gated on the *other* account's status, not on the quality of its own POA. If the related account stays deactivated (abandoned, sold, belongs to a third party who won't appeal, or is unrecoverable), the linked account has no "reinstated related account" to cite and must instead break the link with documents. — [Amazon staff reply](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/02f329bf-fe76-458e-ac70-af671b635e06); [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/)
- [V] Under BSA, funds eligibility looks at "your account—or any other account you have operated" (see §5). An account whose operator also ran a fraud-flagged account carries that history, even though its own POA is identical. — [Amazon BSA PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/rainier/help./1.1_BSA_PDF_English.pdf)
- [P] The strength of the link matters: a shared bank account or tax ID "carries more weight" than a shared Wi-Fi network. — [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/amazon-linked-accounts/)
- [P] An account opened to evade an earlier suspension is treated as circumvention, not as a separate case. — [McCabe on WebRetailer](https://www.webretailer.com/b/amazon-seller-suspensions)

### Inferences
- A tool must ask, before drafting: does the seller know which account? Is it theirs, a former one, a family member's, or a stranger's? What is its current status? Can it be appealed, and has it been? What exactly is shared, and since when? A POA that omits a related account Amazon already knows about risks reading as a false denial.
- The deliverable differs by branch: "the related account is mine and now reinstated" (cite it with a screenshot); "it is mine and cannot be reinstated" (closure and separation evidence); "it is not mine" (identity and control documents explaining the shared fact).

### Gaps
- No official Amazon help page on related-account appeals was retrievable. The staff forum reply is the closest primary source.
- No reliable data on how often a linked account is reinstated while the other stays down.

## 4. VAs, agencies and multi-account service providers

### Takeaway
Practitioners consistently flag shared VAs and agencies as a link. Their advice is to grant access through User Permissions, never shared logins, and to revoke and record access. A seller whose VA or agency served a suspended account can be linked through that provider.

### Cited Findings
- [P] Giving user permissions to someone who had a suspended seller account, or API access to a company whose credentials were revoked, are named triggers. Bryant says to check User Permissions History for revoked access. — [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-related-accounts-suspensions/)
- [P] Working with the same VA, employee or third-party agency is a trigger. A main login shared with a VA who moves between accounts creates IP/device/cookie overlaps. Use Amazon's User Permissions instead of sharing credentials. — [AmazonSellersLawyer](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/related-accounts-amazon-suspension)
- [U] A seller reports being linked through a VA "in another country so we have never logged in on the same device or using the same IP address", which suggests separation of devices is not always a defence (the shared-person link may suffice). — [Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/bb09acea-ade8-47c9-9082-d8d634b368c2)
- [P] Traverse Legal lists "shared employees, service providers" among the signals. — [Traverse Legal](https://www.traverselegal.com/blog/amazon-linked-accounts/)

### Inferences
- A tool should ask for: every person and firm with Seller Central access (current and past, with dates); whether access was through User Permissions or shared credentials; whether any of them worked on an account that was suspended; and any SP-API or third-party apps authorised.

### Gaps
- No Amazon statement was found on how VA- or agency-borne links are evaluated. Riverbend and Seller Candy material on this point was not retrieved.

## 5. Held funds after deactivation, and the BSA and Agent Policy terms

### Takeaway
The BSA lets Amazon withhold payments "for as long as" it judges risk persists. It can withhold "permanently" if the account, "or any other account you have operated", was used for deceptive, fraudulent or illegal activity, including counterfeit. The funds appeal is separate from the reinstatement appeal. The waiting period before a disbursement request is reported as 90 days in older notices and 60 days in a later announcement (practitioners say confirm in the notice). The policy was reportedly renamed from "Funds Withholding Policy" to "Funds Disbursement Eligibility Policy".

### Cited Findings
- [V] BSA Section 2: "If we determine that your actions or performance may result in returns, chargebacks, claims, disputes, violations of our terms or policies, violations of law or other risks to Amazon or third parties, then we may in our sole discretion withhold any payments to you for as long as we determine any related risks to Amazon or third parties persist." — [Amazon BSA PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/rainier/help./1.1_BSA_PDF_English.pdf)
- [V] Same section: "To be eligible for fund disbursement, you must refrain from deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity and remain in compliance with our Program Policies. If we determine that your account—or any other account you have operated—has been used to engage in deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity (including the sale of counterfeit or stolen goods), or your use of the Services has harmed, or our controls identify that it might harm, other sellers, customers, or Amazon's legitimate interests, then we may in our sole discretion permanently withhold any payments to you… We will offer you an avenue to appeal our fund withholding decision… Successfully completing the appeal process is required to be eligible for further disbursements." — same PDF
- [V] Amazon may also offset amounts owed, charge the seller's card, reverse bank credits, and require other amounts to secure performance. — same PDF
- [U, Amazon notice text posted by sellers] "When we deactivate an account, we withhold funds to settle outstanding transactions, including product returns or refunds, A-to-z claims from customers, inventory removal costs and outstanding fees." — [Seller Forums search result](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/187d7fd9-11ec-42b3-80c0-4c2e2123bbe1)
- [U, Amazon notice] "due to your account deactivation, disbursements have been temporarily disabled… You are eligible to request a final disbursement in accordance with our Funds Withholding Policy… Some or all of your funds will remain held to settle outstanding transactions and/or damages… This investigation is separate from a reinstatement appeal." Contact: disbursement-appeals (amazon.co.uk). — [Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/a1034002-ff58-4f01-ac2d-ef82dc73d592)
- [U, Amazon notice] Funds "will not be transferred to you but will stay in your account while we work with you to address this issue. This may take up to 90 days, but funds may be held for longer." — [Seller Forums results](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/4253f319-707d-4e8e-9503-cad8ca21e98b)
- **Disagreement on timing:** [U] "after 90 days from when your account was deactivated, you may separately request funds disbursement by contacting disbursement-appeals@amazon.co.uk" ([SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/64a7c91b34d3aa46b707b20957528961)). Against this, [U] a UK update reportedly "reduced the time within which sellers may appeal for their disbursements from 90 days to 60 days from the date of deactivation" ([search summary of SCE/UK threads](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/740b3b7f-1f00-4a45-b608-4f0890475f6a)). [P] DAM Law Firm (Mark Nathaniel Toledo, 2 Jul 2026) says sellers "generally have 60 days from the date of deactivation to appeal for disbursement" and should confirm in the notice. — [DAM Law Firm](https://damlawfirm.com/blog/amazon-withheld-funds-appeal-guide/). AppealDeck's own deadlines model uses a +60-day funds window (CLAUDE.md, 2 Sep 2026 verification), consistent with the newer figure.
- [P] The policy was renamed from "Fund Withholding Policy" to "Funds Disbursement Eligibility Policy". Ineligibility follows if Amazon concludes the account was used for "deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity, or to repeatedly violate Amazon's Program Policies". Withholding reasons named: inauthenticity, related-account allegations, drop-shipping, tracking problems, manipulation, forged documents, unverified identity/entity/bank. Factors for a release appeal: genuine product, delivered orders, resolved refunds/claims, a documented supply chain, and a balance exceeding customer exposure. Ask for a transaction-level accounting. "Reinstatement and funds release are separate issues." Last resort: AAA arbitration under the BSA. — [DAM Law Firm](https://damlawfirm.com/blog/amazon-withheld-funds-appeal-guide/)
- [U] A disbursement request triggers "a separate investigation to evaluate your account for abusive, fraudulent, or other prohibited activity", sometimes with a video verification call. Sellers are asked to verify identity and supply chain before release; some report no reply. — [Seller Forums results](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/73e756b2-8db4-40de-b718-68f89ce6303f); [SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/7a71d7bc-09b7-43bc-a9c6-bec5e7c0985e)
- [P] Amazon may freeze funds until the seller can authenticate its inventory and/or supply chain. — [AmazonSuspensionLawyer](https://amazonsuspensionlawyer.com/amazon-holding-seller-funds/)
- [P, marketing] "Amazon typically holds funds for 90 days after an account is deactivated" for refunds and chargebacks. — [AmazonSellersLawyer](https://amazonsellerslawyer.com/recover-withheld-amazon-funds). Treat with caution.
- Agent Policy: not fetched this round. Per AppealDeck's 22 Sep 2026 legal research (internal), it has been in force since 4 Mar 2026 and prohibits browser automation and Seller Central scraping. It binds the seller. No source found ties it to funds withholding.

### Inferences
- Three different money states need different questions: (a) the ordinary hold while an appeal runs; (b) a final disbursement request after the window (60 or 90 days) when not reinstated; (c) permanent withholding for fraud or counterfeit, which can be driven by *another account the seller operated*. A tool should ask: deactivation date; exact funds wording in the notice; balance and open claims/refunds/chargebacks; whether any other account the seller operated was flagged for fraud or counterfeit; and whether a separate disbursement request has been sent, with its date.
- Because the window figure varies by marketplace and over time, a tool should read the window from the seller's own notice and never assume one.

### Gaps
- The live Funds Disbursement Eligibility Policy page (US) could not be fetched. The rename and the 60-day figure rest on one law-firm source plus a UK forum report.
- The BSA PDF read is the NA text linked to a 2022 translation. Whether the November 2026 live BSA wording is unchanged was not confirmed.
- The Agent Policy text was not retrieved in this pass.

## 6. Identity verification and re-verification as background-history cases

### Takeaway
Video identity verification is long-standing. A September 2026 sweep reportedly re-verified established accounts too, with listings pulled and disbursements held during review. Failed verification produces Section 3 deactivations, and identity concerns are a named funds-withholding reason.

### Cited Findings
- [P/trade press] Novadata (21 Sep 2026), citing HeyCross (20 Sep) reporting AMZ123, and Maijiazhijia (17 Sep): Amazon ran a centralised review of "roughly 20,000 accounts ahead of peak season" centred on video identity verification. Some sellers were rejected because they "sounded rehearsed rather than like the person running the business". Listings were pulled and disbursements held while the review ran. Established accounts were hit alongside new ones. Amazon published no policy change. The 20,000 figure is trade-press only and unconfirmed by Amazon. — [Novadata](https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-section-3-video-verification-sweep-september-2026)
- [U] Sellers report "Amazon failed to verify my identity after I completed the video verification call", deactivation after a failed interview, scheduling links that do not work, and Amazon refusing a second call. — [SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/73ba0e76-49bf-45d6-a736-17cd9218d133); [UK thread](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/93aefa9e-4397-468c-bf1a-1f32f2070086); [SCE thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/191b2b0e-10dd-47d9-8172-680e5e8d8239)
- [U] Repeated verification across deactivations ("Deactivated For 3rd time, Amazon repeated Video Identity Verification"). — [Seller Forums MX](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.mx/seller-forums/discussions/t/8e884d7b-a9bd-418e-aea1-6f203c31d76c)
- [P] Unverified account holder, business entity or bank information is named as a withholding reason. Preserve video verification notices and identity records. — [DAM Law Firm](https://damlawfirm.com/blog/amazon-withheld-funds-appeal-guide/)

### Inferences
- Verification cases overlap with related-account cases. The documents that prove identity (ID, proof of address, bank statement) are the same ones that break a link. A person who cannot pass verification as "the person running the business", for example because a VA or agency actually runs it, also points to a people link.
- A tool should ask: prior verification history (dates, outcome), who appears on the call versus who operates the account, and whether the names on the ID, bank and business documents match.

### Gaps
- Amazon's official identity-verification help page was not fetched. No primary Amazon confirmation of the September 2026 sweep exists.

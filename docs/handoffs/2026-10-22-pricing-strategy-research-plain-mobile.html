The short answer

Do not make suspended sellers pay monthly. Sell the same seller in more ways, and get the monthly money from appeal writers and VA agencies.

  • One offer is not a money modelHormozi's model is a chain: something that pulls people in, something bigger, something smaller for those who say no, and something that repeats. Today we have only the $249 Pass.
  • A one-time need is not a subscriptionHormozi says it in plain words: someone with a one-time need should not pay forever. A suspended seller buys once, in panic.
  • The repeat money is professionalsWriters and VAs handle cases every week. That is where a monthly plan makes sense. No tool for them was found.
  • Partners, not ads, bring buyersA writer who sends a seller gets 30% of the Pass. Each such sale still pays for itself more than twice over in its first month. Ads stay closed at any price software can charge.

What is wrong with the offer today

Hormozi's value formula: the result the buyer wants, times how sure they are of getting it, divided by how long it takes and how much work it is.

  • "How sure they are" is weakNo reviews, an unknown one-person company, no outcome numbers. Agencies show hundreds of reviews.
  • "How much work" is highThe seller gathers, checks, writes and sends. An agency does it for them.
  • The free part gives away too muchFree should show the problem and point to the next one. Today it also checks invoices, which is part of the cure. Keep one free document check as a sample; the rest goes in the Pass.
  • Nothing for the seller who says no, and nothing afterNo cheaper option, no bigger option, no reason to come back.

The plan for sellers

1. Pull them in

  • Free decoder stays freePlus one free document check, then: "Now each record has to show the right things."
  • Founding price: $149 for the first 25 paid cases, then $249A real, public counter. The buyer agrees to report the outcome. Never tied to a good review (the US bans that).
  • $25 back for an outcome reportWithin 120 days. It pays for the outcome numbers we have never had.

2. Two passes side by side

Case PassThis case, every round, until Amazon decides
$249
Account PassThis case plus every new Amazon notice on the same account for 12 months
$329

Only $80 more for a year of cover, so most people should pick it. An agency charges about $1,000 for one listing reinstatement. Above the prices, a true dated line: "Writers $50–$600. Agencies $795–$2,500 per case."

Give the existing extras names (the evidence pack, the deadline clock, the reply reader, the invoice-against-sales check) and call the 14-day refund a money-back guarantee. The law allows that word because it is a full refund on request. It must never sit next to a promise about reinstatement.

3. For the seller who says no

  • Pay in partsTurn on card and PayPal "pay later" where Paddle offers it. Paddle pays us in full; no risk.
  • Document Check, $59Up to five documents checked, no response writing. The full $59 counts toward a Pass within 30 days.

4. After the case

  • Renew the Account Pass for $99 a yearA seller suspended once is the likeliest to get the next notice.

The plan for writers and VA agencies

First month $1. Yearly = ten months' price. We sell only the software; the writer bills the client himself, which keeps us inside Paddle's rules (Paddle bans selling human services like consulting or legal help).

Writers who do not want a plan can still send clients: 30% of each Pass to the writer, $25 off for the seller, paid by Payoneer at first.

Do not do

  • A monthly plan for suspended sellersWrong by Hormozi, and it puts us beside the free AI tools on price.
  • "Pay 80% after reinstatement"Paddle cannot collect it later, we cannot check the result, and the buyer can simply not pay.
  • Money back if Amazon refuses, for nowLegal if every condition sits beside it, but card refunds stop after 120 days and we do not know how often Amazon refuses. Revisit after 30 outcome reports, as a $49 add-on.
  • Fake countdowns or "only 3 left"Banned in the US, EU and UK unless true. The founding counter must be real.
  • Agency prices for softwareNot believable without a human, and we cannot sell the human through Paddle.

The order: one step at a time

Hormozi: get one offer working before adding the next, and judge each over months, not weeks.

  1. Now: founding price, named extras and guarantee, the market price line, the $25 outcome rebate, one free document check, the 30% partner code.
  2. After 25 paid cases: price to $249, the Account Pass at $329, pay later, the linked-account add-on ($99).
  3. When 3 writers ask to pay: the Solo, Studio and Agency plans.
  4. After 50 paid cases and 30 outcome reports: the $59 Document Check; raise to $299, then $349, while money per 50 started cases keeps rising.
  5. Month 11 of the first Account Passes: the $99 yearly renewal.
Rough effect (an estimate, not a forecast)If 6 in 10 buyers pick the Account Pass, average money per buyer goes from about $236 to about $281 after Paddle's fee, up 19%, before any renewals or writer plans.

# Growth and pricing blueprint: roles, offers, partners, retention (22 Oct 2026)

> **What this is.** The second, deeper pass the founder asked for on 22 Oct 2026: Hormozi's _$100M Money Models_ in detail **plus** the other best books on pricing, marketing and retention, turned into one structured system with role-based sign-up, a partner ("mediator") program and a retention loop for each kind of customer. It builds on and **corrects** `2026-10-22-pricing-strategy-research.md` (same day) where the wider reading changed the answer (§1). Research and design only; nothing is built. Plain-words version: `2026-10-22-growth-and-pricing-blueprint-plain-mobile.html`.
>
> As instructed, existing pricing decisions and rules do not limit the design; outside facts do (Paddle's rules, consumer law, what the code can do). Tags as in the research file: **V** read on a source today, **S** snippet, **E** my estimate, **U** unverified.

## 0. The system on one screen

| Who signs up                                          | What they buy                                                                             | Why they come back                                          | How they bring others                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Seller** (account in trouble)                       | Free decode → **Case Pass $249** or **Account Pass $329** (12 months of every new notice) | Amazon's next reply, the next notice, the $99/yr renewal    | $25 off a friend, $25 back                              |
| **Professional** (appeal writer, consultant, VA team) | Free Pro workspace → **case credits** (client pays, wholesale packs, or **Pro $299/mo**)  | Every new client case; credits each month; the case history | Uses AppealDeck on clients' cases                       |
| **Partner** (trainer, community, creator, accountant) | Nothing; they **earn**                                                                    | Monthly payout; tier upgrades                               | Link + code: 30% of each Pass, 20% of Pro for 12 months |

**One rule runs through all of it: every case costs about the same, whoever pays.** That stops a seller signing up as a "writer" to get a cheap case, which is the leak most role-based pricing has (Simon: a price fence only holds when the value gap is real).

## 1. What the wider reading changed in this morning's plan

| This morning                           | Now                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Founding **price** $149 for 25 cases   | Founding **bonus**: the first 25 buyers get the Account Pass at the Case Pass price                                        | Hormozi himself: "never discount the core, add bonuses" (V). Simon: a buyer who cannot judge quality reads a low price as a weak product; a panicking seller is exactly that buyer (V). Same scarcity, no cheap signal. |
| Writer plans $49 / $149 / $399 a month | **Client pays** (partner earns 30%), **wholesale packs** for verified pros, and one **Pro $299/mo** plan with case credits | A $49 "writer" plan is a $49 case for any seller who ticks "writer": it would cannibalize the $249 Pass. Campbell: price on the value metric, and for both buyers that is the **case** (V).                             |
| A guarantee stack with a vivid name    | Keep the 14-day money-back; no outcome promise; no invented bonus "value" totals                                           | Ramanujam caps a line at under 4 products and 9 benefits; a panicking buyer needs fewer, clearer reasons (V). Outcome claims need evidence under FTC v. DoNotPay (V).                                                   |
| Downsells by price and by shape        | Downsell by **shape** (Document Check, pay-later), not by cutting the Pass                                                 | Ramanujam: three non-price actions before any price cut; Campbell: discounts are "a sledgehammer instead of a scalpel" (V/S).                                                                                           |
| Writer monthly seats                   | One Pro plan whose monthly fee is **at or above** the Pass                                                                 | Same fence as above; Hormozi's waived-fee logic gives the commitment.                                                                                                                                                   |

Everything else in the morning file stands (no seller subscription, no pay-after-reinstatement, no outcome refund yet, ads closed, one stage at a time).

## 2. The frameworks behind each part

| Part of the system      | Framework (book)                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The offer chain         | Money model: attraction → upsell → downsell → continuity; level-3 client-financed acquisition (Hormozi, _Money Models_)                                                                     |
| What the offer is worth | Value equation; "fast beats free"; bonuses answer objections (Hormozi, _Offers_)                                                                                                            |
| Who to sell what        | Segment by willingness to pay; leaders / fillers / killers; good-better-best with under 4 options (Ramanujam, _Monetizing Innovation_)                                                      |
| How the price reads     | Price signals quality under doubt; fences; value split (Simon); decoy and magnetic middle; zero-price effect; anchoring (Ariely, Poundstone)                                                |
| What to charge on       | Value metric = the case (Campbell / ProfitWell)                                                                                                                                             |
| What we say             | Competitive alternatives first (Dunford); hero, guide, 3-step plan, stakes (Miller, StoryBrand); reciprocity, commitment, unity (Cialdini)                                                  |
| Where buyers come from  | Lead getters: affiliates, agencies, referrals; Rule of 100 (Hormozi, _Leads_); bullseye, test 3 channels, keep 1 (Weinberg, _Traction_); practical value spreads (Berger, _Contagious_)     |
| Who we learn from first | Past behaviour and past spending, not opinions (Fitzpatrick, _The Mom Test_)                                                                                                                |
| Why people come back    | Trigger → action → reward → investment, for professionals only (Eyal, _Hooked_); superusers (Baxter); a recurring fee needs a recurring outcome (Tzuo); define the outcome you own (Murphy) |
| How partners are run    | Referral path know → like → trust → try → buy → refer (Jantsch); partners sit in the moments before purchase (McBain); FTC endorsement duties                                               |

Where the books disagree with Hormozi, this blueprint takes the side that fits a frightened one-time buyer: no manufactured urgency, no outcome guarantee yet, continuity only where the need repeats.

## 3. Roles at sign-up

**One optional question**, at sign-up and on `/pricing`: _"What brings you here?"_

| Choice shown                      | Role         | Default pricing view | Default home                           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| "My Amazon account has a problem" | `seller`     | For sellers          | The case                               |
| "I write appeals for clients"     | `pro`        | For professionals    | All cases (client names)               |
| "I run a VA team or agency"       | `pro` (team) | For professionals    | All cases, team note                   |
| "I send sellers to good tools"    | `partner`    | For partners         | Partner sign-up (the affiliate portal) |
| "Just looking" / skip             | none         | All plans            | The decoder                            |

Rules (from the role-based onboarding research, V/S): one question, skippable; the role **personalizes, never gates**; a "See all plans" switch on every pricing view; role changeable in settings. The vendor-reported lifts (Guru +71% activation, Make +4–6%) are marketing claims; measure our own.

`/pricing` gets three tabs (**Sellers · Professionals · Partners**), opened on the visitor's role, all visible.

## 4. Sellers: the offer chain

| Step       | Offer                                                                                                | Price       | Framework                                                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attraction | Free decoder, scam check, records list, Amazon's reply read, **one** document check                  | $0          | Lead magnet that reveals the next problem; zero-price effect; reciprocity |
| Attraction | **Founding bonus:** first 25 buyers get the Account Pass for $249 (public counter, true)             | —           | Real scarcity, bonus not discount                                         |
| Core       | **Case Pass**: this case, every round, until Amazon decides                                          | **$249**    | Price signals quality; value metric = case                                |
| Upsell     | **Account Pass**: the case + every new Amazon notice on the same account for 12 months               | **$329**    | Magnetic middle; Buy X Get Y Free                                         |
| Upsell     | Linked account added at checkout (when the notice names related accounts)                            | $99 each    | Classic upsell at the moment the problem appears                          |
| Downsell   | Pay in parts (card / PayPal Pay Later through Paddle; paid in full to us)                            | same        | Payment plan, zero default risk                                           |
| Downsell   | **Document Check**: up to 5 documents read against the notice; $59 credited to a Pass within 30 days | $59         | Feature downsell + rollover (Pass ≥ 4× the credit)                        |
| Continuity | Account Cover renewal at month 11                                                                    | $99/year    | Continuity where the risk repeats                                         |
| Data       | $25 back for an outcome report within 120 days                                                       | −$25        | Win Your Money Back on actions, not on Amazon's decision                  |
| Referral   | Friend gets $25 off, you get $25 back                                                                | −$25 / −$25 | Customer referrals (Hormozi, Jantsch)                                     |

**Kept true:** the 14-day refund, called a money-back guarantee (lawful as a full refund on request, 16 CFR 239.3), never beside a reinstatement claim.

## 5. Professionals: the pro ladder

Appeal writers charge $5 to $600 per POA (most about $50); agencies $795 to $2,500 (V/S). Both earn **per case**, so they pay per case too.

| Route                     | How it works                                                                                               | Price to the pro                                           |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pro workspace**         | Free for verified pros: all cases in one list with client names, client-facing export, the fact lock stamp | $0                                                         |
| **Client pays**           | The pro sends the client a checkout link; the client pays $249 (or $224 with the pro's code)               | Pro **earns** 30% (about $75)                              |
| **Wholesale packs**       | Verified pros buy case credits and bill clients as part of their own fee; credits last 12 months           | 5 for **$870** ($174 each) · 20 for **$2,990** ($150 each) |
| **Pro plan (continuity)** | 3 case credits a month, unused credits roll over one month, 5 logins, own logo on exports                  | **$299/month**; extra credits $99; yearly = 10 months      |

- **The fence:** every route costs a seller at least as much as a Pass for one case, and wholesale needs verification (a public profile: Fiverr, Upwork, LinkedIn or a site). A seller gains nothing by pretending.
- **Before fixing these numbers:** ask 10 pros Ramanujam's questions ("acceptable price per case? expensive? too expensive? why?") and the Mom Test ones ("walk me through your last case; what did you pay for tools?"). Count only a real case run through the tool as a yes. The prices above are opening positions.
- **Not sold:** the pro's own writing. Paddle bars human services (V); the pro bills the client directly.
- **Code limit (V, repo):** cases live encrypted on one device. Five logins can each work their own cases; **sharing one case between team members needs cases stored on our server**, which changes the privacy promise. v1: no shared cases; hand a case over by encrypted export.

## 6. Partners ("mediators")

Who: training communities (Enablers, Ecommerce Success Pakistan, local institutes), YouTubers and course creators, accountants and bookkeepers, VA agencies that do not want to write appeals, and pros who refer instead of handling.

| Tier         | Reached at                   | Earns                                                         | Gets                                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Referrer     | Sign-up                      | **30% of each Pass** (one time); **20% of Pro** for 12 months | Link, code ($25 off for their seller), live dashboard, ready posts        |
| Partner      | 5 paid referrals             | Same                                                          | Co-branded landing page, directory listing, a free Pro workspace          |
| Lead partner | 20 paid referrals in 90 days | Same 30% (perks, not a higher rate)                           | First look at new features, a monthly call with the founder, a named page |

- **Cookie:** 60 days, first click (a case can take weeks before the buy; norm at Jungle Scout and SmartScout, V/S).
- **Benchmarks:** Amazon-seller tools pay 20–30% recurring, mostly for 12 months (Helium 10, Jungle Scout, SmartScout, SellerApp, V); one-time digital products 30–50% (S).
- **Tool:** **Tolt** (Paddle Billing supported per its help centre, names Payoneer for payouts; $99/mo Growth for automatic payouts) or **FirstPromoter** ($49/mo, Paddle Billing V, no Payoneer). **Not Rewardful**: Paddle Classic only (V). Start manual on Tolt Basic ($69) with Payoneer-to-Payoneer payouts (free inside Payoneer, S).
- **Paddle:** running an affiliate program for our own software is not "selling a referral program"; never sell a partner membership or "community access" (both prohibited, V).
- **Legal duties (V):** partners say "I get a commission" beside the link (FTC); advisers (writers, accountants) tell the seller about the fee in writing; we give partners a short list of claims they may not make (no "guarantee" of reinstatement, no win rates, no "Amazon partner") and check their posts each quarter (16 CFR 255.1(d)); never reward a review for being positive (16 CFR 465).
- **Unit check:** 30% of $249 = $74.70; we keep about $161 after Paddle; 30-day profit is 2.16× the cost of the customer (Hormozi level 3). At 35% we would keep about $149 against $87, only 1.7×, which is why the top tier gets perks rather than a higher rate.

## 7. Retention: why each role comes back

| Role         | Trigger                                | Reason to stay                                                                               | Measure                                               |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Seller       | Amazon's reply; a new notice; month 11 | Every round included; Account Pass covers the next notice; the case history is already there | Rounds per case; Account Pass take rate; renewal rate |
| Seller       | Outcome reached                        | $25 for the outcome report; rollover to Account Cover within 30 days                         | Outcome reports per 10 Passes                         |
| Professional | Each new client notice                 | Credits each month; every past case and its documents in one place (investment, Eyal)        | Month-2 and month-6 retention; credits used           |
| Professional | Superusers (Baxter)                    | The founder answers them first, in Urdu if they like; early features; a named page           | Cases per pro per month                               |
| Partner      | Monthly payout; tier upgrade           | Real money, a co-branded page, recognition                                                   | Share of Passes from partners                         |

**The outcome AppealDeck owns (Murphy):** "a complete response: every requested record answered or honestly declared". Amazon's decision is not ours to promise; this one is, and it is what the product should count and show.

## 8. What we say (positioning and pages)

- **Alternatives (Dunford):** writing it alone, ChatGPT, a $50 writer, a $1,000+ agency. **The one thing none of them does:** read the seller's own documents against Amazon's notice, every round.
- **Home (StoryBrand):** the seller is the hero; AppealDeck is the guide; the plan is three steps already in the product (paste the notice → gather the records → send the response); the stakes are real (held money, a closing window); the transitional call is "Decode a notice free".
- **Grunt test:** in five seconds a visitor knows what it is, how it helps, what to click.
- **Proof without reviews (Cialdini):** the open test results, the founder's own note, the dated market price strip, the outcome ledger as it fills (with its denominator).
- **Spreadable (Berger):** the scam check and the decoder are the things a group admin forwards; make each result shareable without the seller's data.

## 9. Where buyers come from (the first 90 days)

Weinberg's bullseye with Hormozi's Rule of 100, scaled to one person:

| Channel                               | Daily or weekly action                                                                | Kept if, after 30 days                 |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Business development (pros, VAs)      | 20 personal messages a day in English or Urdu; 10 Mom Test calls in the first 2 weeks | 3 pros ran a real case                 |
| Free tools (engineering as marketing) | The decoder and scam check, linked in answers                                         | 50 decodes a week from outside         |
| Community notice clinic               | Answer 5 "my account was suspended" posts a day in existing groups, as a member       | 10 decodes a week traced to the clinic |

Keep the one that works; drop the other two (Traction). Ads stay off: a $9.87 click and a 0.5–2% buy rate cost $490 to $1,970 per buyer against $236 of cash (R, 21 Sep).

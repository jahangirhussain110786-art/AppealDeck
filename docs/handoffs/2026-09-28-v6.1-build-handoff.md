# v6.1 build handoff — the case as five plain steps (approved 28 Sep 2026)

## 1. In plain words (for the founder)

- **Approved:** prototype v6.1 (`docs/handoffs/2026-09-28-prototype-v6.1-simple-case/`). The founder approved it in chat on 28 Sep 2026 with one condition: **nothing is missed, deleted or removed** — everything is kept, re-organised and re-worded.
- **What gets built:** the case page becomes a home plus 5 steps, in the v5 design. The steps are:
  1. check what Amazon wants
  2. gather your documents
  3. write your answers
  4. check your letter
  5. send it to Amazon
- **Nothing is locked:** the seller can open any step at any time. Every sentence is re-written in short, plain words.
- **Nothing disappears.** The prototype draws only one kind of case (a Plan of Action, first round). The real page has about 120 features and states, and every one of them is listed in [the inventory](2026-09-28-case-page-inventory.md). §4 below gives each one its place in the 5 steps. The build is finished only when every inventory line has been checked off.
- **Nothing is sent anywhere new, nothing about pricing changes, and no feature is added or removed.** This is re-organisation and wording only.
- **Four small choices** are in §6, each with the choice already made. The build does not wait for them; the founder can change any of them.

## 2. Hard rules for the build

1. **Every inventory line survives.** It may be re-worded, moved into a step, or folded behind "Change it" / "(?)" / "I don't have it" / "More". It may not be deleted. If something truly cannot be kept, stop and ask the founder; do not decide it.
2. **Same data, same rules.** No change to `src/core/*`, the vault format, `workspaceSchema.ts`, API routes, the Pass/entitlement checks, the gap rules, or what leaves the browser.
   - The steps are a new _view_ of the existing workspace state.
   - A step's status is computed from existing fields (§5).
3. **The honest lines stay, shorter.** "Nothing is sent to Amazon / you send it yourself", "We added this" / "You added this", "We can't check this" (attestation), "Nobody can promise Amazon will say yes", "This describes your document only…", what a document check and wording help send, and the gated (D6) professional-help routing.
   - `src/content/__tests__/legalDisclosures.test.ts` pins some of these.
   - Re-word them only so that the meaning is identical, and update the test in the same commit.
4. **Old links keep working.** `?view=overview|evidence|response|history` must still land somewhere sensible: home, step 2, step 3 (or 4 once prepared), and step 5. New links use `?step=1..5`.
5. **Copy lives in `src/content/`** (`workspace.ts`, `app.ts`). The existing `lint:copy` rules apply: no "guarantee", no win rates or bare percentages, no exclamation marks.
6. **Writing rules** (prototype v6 index):
   - short sentences, one idea each;
   - say "you" and "we";
   - start buttons with a verb;
   - say "documents" not "records/evidence", and "letter" not "response facts";
   - explain an Amazon word once;
   - give every question an example;
   - give Seller Central directions click by click.
7. **Time labels are estimates.** Write them as "about 10 min", never as a promise.

## 5. Step status and each kind of case

**Status of each step** (computed from existing state; no new fields):

- **Step 1:** done when the route is confirmed and still matches the notice. If the notice changed, it goes back to "Check again".
- **Step 2:** "x of y documents". Done when every record is reviewed or declared cannot-obtain **and** the all-records tick is on.
- **Step 3:** done when the route's response gaps are clear. In progress once any text exists.
- **Step 4:** done when the letter is prepared and the final review tick is on.
- **Step 5:** done when a submission is recorded this round. If a reply is pending, it shows "Amazon replied".
- **"Start here"** follows the NextStepCard priority.

## 6. Four small choices (made; the founder can change them)

1. **The reviewed tick becomes the save button** ("Save: I checked this file"). Pressing it is the same statement as ticking the box; the save still requires the file and the note. _Alternative: keep a separate tick above the button._
2. **Before the Pass, step 4 shows the letter's sections with ✓ written / missing**, not the assembled text. After the Pass and "Build my letter", it shows the full letter with the orange gaps. This keeps today's rule that the prepared draft is the paid part. _Alternative: show the seller's own words assembled before payment._
3. **Verification cases show "no letter" in step 4** and use step 5 for "do it in Seller Central + tell us". The seller still sees 5 steps, for consistency.
4. **The decode result gets the three small wording changes** from the v6 index:
   - "Open case workspace" → "Start my 5 steps";
   - "Your notice, marked up" → "Your notice, with the important parts marked";
   - "What to gather: 2 records" → "Documents you'll need: 2".

   `e2e/access.spec.ts` and `decode-continuity.spec.ts` assert some of these names; update them in the same commit.

## 7. Build order (batched, per the founder's standing rule: 3–4 tasks per commit, gates once per batch, push when green)

2. **Structure.**

   - the side card cut to "Reply by";
   - the step status computation (§5) as a pure, unit-tested helper in `src/lib/` (not `src/core`).
3. **Step 1** (one-tap summary + "Change it" panel + scam warning and multi-issue kept visible) and **step 2** (one document open at a time, "(?)", "I don't have it" menu, the merged save, facts ledger).
4. **Step 3** (one question per screen, per route) and **step 4** (gaps inside the letter, the Pass/sign-in gates unchanged, build + review + copy).
5. **Step 5** (send checklist + copy + record + replies/delta/history/downloads), the phone Next bar, and the time labels.

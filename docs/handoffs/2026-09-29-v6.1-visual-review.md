# v6.1 visual review: prototype against the real app (29 Sep 2026)

Before building v6.1, the founder asked for one more check: are there visual improvements, small or large, that the build should also make?

**Method.** I took headless screenshots of all six v6.1 pages and of the real `/case` page. The real page used a guest case built from the sample notice, shown on every tab, at 1440 px and 390 px, in light and dark. I compared them side by side, then checked each finding against the code. This review adds to [the build handoff](2026-09-28-v6.1-build-handoff.md). It does not replace it. Every item has a default choice, so the build does not wait. The founder can change any of them.

## In plain words

- **The prototype is right about the big things.** A case home, 5 steps, one decision per screen, gaps shown inside the letter. Nothing here changes that.
- **In 4 places the prototype, copied exactly, would make the app less honest.** They are listed in §1, and the build must not copy them.
- **12 small visual improvements** make the steps clearer. Most of them remove repetition. They are in §2.
- **Phones need 5 fixes** the prototype does not show. The step bar is cut off, and the bottom bar covers buttons. They are in §3.
- **The prototype has no dark mode.** Its colours must be matched to the app's colour tokens, and 4 surfaces need a dark version designed. §4.
- **6 accessibility details**, §5.
- **5 small problems already in the app** can be fixed in the same pass, §6.
- **Things the app has and the prototype leaves out stay** (Pricing in the menu, theme switch, Sign in, New case). §7.

## 2. Visual improvements to make while building

1. **Say "done" once.** A finished step row currently shows a green tick, the word "Done" underneath, and a "Done" pill. Keep the tick and the pill. Use the line underneath for what was decided, e.g. "Plan of Action, 2 documents".
2. **Say "Step 2 of 5" once.** On step pages it appears in the breadcrumb, in the eyebrow and in the rail. Keep the rail and the eyebrow. The breadcrumb reads "Cases / Policy violation", as it does on the home.
3. **Keep the deadline in view on every step.** The "Reply by" card exists only on the home. A seller working in step 2 cannot see the date. Put a small `DeadlineChip` (already built) at the right of the step header, on every step page.
4. **Use the empty right side on wide screens.** Step pages are 760 px, left-aligned, leaving about 400 px blank at 1440. Default: keep the column (it is the easiest width to read). On step 2, use the right side for a sticky list of the documents with their status, so the one-open-at-a-time cards stay findable. Other steps keep it empty.
5. **One orange button per screen.** Step 2 shows "Save: I checked this file" and "Next: write your answers" both in orange, and the phone bar adds a third. Inside a document card, Save is orange. The page's "Next" becomes the light style while that card still has unsaved input.
6. **The home hero's right half is empty.** The prototype removed the next-record preview, but handoff §4 keeps it on desktop. Put the existing `NextRecordPaper` back in the hero, which also balances it.
7. **The hero pill repeats the breadcrumb** ("Policy violation" twice). Use the pill for the route instead ("Plan of Action"), which is new information.
8. **Time labels look like buttons.** The grey time pill sits next to the "Start here" pill with the same shape. Show time as plain small text ("about 10 min", handoff rule 7) and keep the pill shape for status only.
9. **Question dots with no labels (step 3).** Keep "Question 1 of 3". Make each dot a button named after its question, so a seller can jump back without pressing Back twice.
10. **Answer boxes grow as you type** (step 3). A fixed 170 px box makes long answers scroll inside a scroll. Start at 170 px and grow to the text, with the character count kept underneath.
11. **Checklist ticks in step 5 must not look like saved progress.** If they are not saved, a seller who comes back finds them empty and may think they sent nothing. Default: save them with the round, in the workspace UI state already stored in the vault. If that needs a schema change, make them plain numbered steps instead. Rule 2 forbids schema changes in this pass.
12. **The home's "Amazon replied? Paste the reply here" is a plain sentence under the list.** When a reply is pending it becomes the hero (handoff §4). Otherwise give it a small light button so it doesn't read as a footnote.

## 3. Phones (390 px)

1. **The step rail is cut off** ("Gather your docu…"), with no sign that it scrolls. Under 640 px, show only the current step as "Step 2 of 5 · Gather your documents", with ‹ › buttons and a "All steps" link to the home. When the full rail is shown, scroll the current step into view.
2. **The bottom Next bar covers content.** In step 2 it hides "I don't have it". Give the page bottom padding equal to the bar's height plus the safe area.
3. **Step 3 has no bottom bar**, although every other step does. Put Back / Next question there.
4. **The app's own tabs have the same cut-off today** ("History" is clipped at 390). The rail replaces them, so fixing item 1 fixes both.
5. **The phone top bar** must keep Sign in and Pricing (see §7). The prototype's bar shows only New case / Dashboard / Vault.

## 4. Dark mode and colours

The prototype CSS has no dark mode at all: `v5.css` has no `prefers-color-scheme` or `.dark` rules. v6/v6.1 add 15 distinct hard-coded colours. "No new colours" (handoff §3) therefore means mapping each one to an existing token.

| Prototype colour                                                   | Token                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `#fff` (cards, rail pills, menu options, checklist rows)           | `bg-card`                                            |
| `#f1f1ed`, `#f4f3ee`, `#f7f6f1`, `#fcfbf8`, `#fbfaf7` (soft fills) | `bg-muted` / `bg-surface-1`                          |
| `#e1dfd7`, `#e3e1d9`, `#d6d3c8`, `#c6c9d0`, `#c9c7be` (lines)      | `border` / `ring-border`                             |
| `#fffaf5` ("Start here" row), `#fff7f0` (letter gaps)              | `bg-primary/5` · `bg-primary/10` with `ring-primary` |
| `#1d4ed8` (links)                                                  | `text-link`                                          |
| `#15803d` (ticks)                                                  | `text-success` / `accent-success`                    |

Four surfaces need their dark version checked, not just inherited:

- the "Start here" row tint;
- the orange letter gaps, whose text must pass `contrast.test.ts` as `--primary-ink` on the tint;
- the phone bottom bar's `rgba(250,250,247,0.94)` blur, which becomes `bg-background/90`;
- the (?) help panel.

Add the gap tint pairing to `src/app/__tests__/contrast.test.ts` in the same commit.

The white "paper" preview in the dark hero is deliberate (`CaseOverview.tsx:107` forces `light`). Keep it.

## 5. Accessibility

1. **The Copy button sits inside a checkbox label** (step 5, "Paste your letter"). Clicking Copy also ticks the box, and a button inside a label breaks for screen readers. Make the Copy button a sibling of the label.
2. **The (?) circle is 18 px**, below the 24 px target size in WCAG 2.2. The whole summary line is the target, so give it a minimum height of 24 px.
3. **When Next question changes the screen**, move focus to the new question's heading and announce "Question 2 of 3".
4. **The "I don't have it" menu** uses `<details>`. That is fine, but the options must be buttons (they change state), not `href="#"` links.
5. **Rail links** keep `aria-current="step"` (as the prototype has) and include the status in their name, e.g. "Step 1, done: Check what Amazon wants".
6. **The "Done" pill is green on green**, so the step's state must not be carried by colour alone. The tick icon and the word already cover this. Keep both.

## 6. Small problems already in the app (fix in the same pass)

1. **"Round 1"** renders in a monospace font next to the case title (`CaseWorkspace.tsx:1164`), and looks like leftover debug text. Default: hide it in round 1, and show it as a small pill from round 2 ("Round 2").
2. **Disabled buttons look like faded orange** ("Save this date", "Save business details", "Save evidence review"), which reads as broken. Use the neutral disabled style, and put the reason on the button or under it (handoff §4 already asks this for "Yes, that's right").
3. **"Changes saved"** in the top bar becomes "Saved" (handoff §4 wording). Make sure it never shows before the first real write. The prototype shows "Saved" on an untouched case.
4. **The home side column** holds 3 cards: Timeline, the date field, and No files / Storage / All cases. It becomes the single "Reply by" card plus one line (handoff §4). Put the Timeline events in step 5's history, as planned.
5. **The prototype sidebar stops at the first screen height** on long pages (step 2 and step 4 at 1440). The app's sidebar is already full height. Keep the app's, and don't copy the prototype's fixed height.

## 7. The app has these and the prototype leaves them out: keep them

- **The sidebar menu:** Decode · Dashboard · Vault · Pricing (AM-25, never reopened). The prototype says "Decode a new notice" and drops Pricing.
- **Top bar:** "New case" with its confirm, the theme switch, and Sign in / profile.
- **The sidebar guest card wording:** the prototype's "Saved in this browser · Sign in to keep it safe" is shorter and fine. Keep what the card does, which is link to sign-in with `?next=`.
- **The logo:** the app's navy tile. The prototype draws a white tile, and the logo must not change.

## 8. How to re-check

- Build first, then start `appealdeck-prod`.
- Start the new `prototype-handoffs` launch entry, which serves `docs/handoffs/` on port 4403 so the prototype's `../` links work.
- The scratch script that took these screenshots created a guest case from `SAMPLE_NOTICE_TEXT`, clicked "Confirm this route", and then captured each `?view=`. After the build, use the same steps with `?step=1..5` to compare page by page.

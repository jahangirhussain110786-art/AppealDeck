# SEO pass, 24 Oct 2026

**Honest first line:** no one can promise "first on Google". A new domain starts with no authority, and the pages that rank today for these searches are law firms and agencies that have been published for years. What can be controlled is: (1) a page exists for each problem sellers really type, (2) it is readable by search engines, (3) Google is told about it. Position then depends on time and on links from other sites.

## What was checked

- Google autocomplete (what people really type, not volumes) for 14 seller-problem phrases on 24 Oct 2026. Strong signals: "amazon seller account suspended" + appeal / reinstatement / counterfeit / section 3 / reddit; "amazon appeal denied"; "amazon account health at risk"; "amazon performance notification"; "amazon restricted products policy violations"; "amazon related account suspension"; "amazon account under review"; "seller account deactivated how to reactivate".
- Weak or empty: "authenticity complaint supplier invoice" (nobody types it; they type "counterfeit" or "inauthentic"), funds on hold, ASIN suspended.
- Existing pages already covered Section 3, Plan of Action (+ examples), identity verification, IP complaint, two comparison pages. Earlier research (25 Sep) is in `2026-09-25-keyword-research.md`.

## What was added

- Three guides, each one problem, each handing over to the decoder: `/guides/appeal-denied`, `/guides/inauthentic-complaint`, `/guides/account-at-risk` (`src/content/guidesSearch.ts`). They are in the sitemap, the guides index and the decoder's list automatically. Facts used are only ones the project had already verified; forum-only facts are said to be forum-sourced; no outcome, rate or guarantee.
- Article structured data on guides now carries publisher, image, main page and language; the home page gains a `WebSite` entry.
- `docs/LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` §5b: Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, sitemap submission, and the real `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` before the production build.

## Already in place (checked, left alone)

Unique title and description on every public page (length-tested), per-page canonical, sitemap with real dates only, robots, `noindex` on the signed-in app and sign-in pages, Open Graph and Twitter cards with an image, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data, plain server-rendered text.

## Not done, and why

- **Keyword volumes**: need Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads login). Founder option; autocomplete depth was used instead.
- **Backlinks**: the biggest ranking factor for a new site and not a code task. The writer/VA channel and honest forum answers are the realistic source (`2026-10-12-community-and-reach-research.md`).
- **Restricted products, related accounts, account under review guides**: demand exists in autocomplete but the product does not yet do anything specific for them; a guide that only repeats general advice would not earn a ranking. Add when the notice families get dedicated handling, or after Search Console shows which queries already bring people.
- **Ranking check**: the site is on a test host, so there is nothing to measure until the real domain is live and indexed.

## 1. Dastan: what the record shows

- **One business, four names.** dastantraders.com 301-redirects to dastan.uk (V). The site uses "Dastan Amazon Services" (header), "Dastan Amazon Agency" (footer), "Dastan Traders Ltd" (founder card) and "Dastan Traders LLC" (a US document link) (V).
- **The only public record starts on 2 Oct 2025.** Companies House: DASTAN TRADERS LTD, company 16757454, Active, incorporated 2 October 2025, registered office 35 New Zealand Square, Derby DE22 3BZ (a residential street), SIC 47910 / 70229 / 82990; one director and 75%+ owner, nationality Pakistani, resident in the UK, born June 1995; identity verified 5 Oct 2026 (V). A New Mexico LLC 0008077280 with a commercial registered agent also exists (V). The site's "Since 2019" rests on the founder's own statement; no independent record before October 2025 was found (U).
- **The team page.** The founder is named only as "Dastan" with no surname; the page says "A team of 15 specialists" and lists 22 names with one-line roles, no photos, no cities, no countries; the only photo is the founder's, twice (V). Whether the people are employed, contracted or listed is not knowable from the site (E). The founder's reading that the team is Pakistani cannot be checked against the site; the only nationality on record is the director's at Companies House (V).
- **What they sell.** Twenty services from Seller Central setup to full account management; reinstatement is two of them (listing and account). No price, package or hourly rate on any page (V). The model: "We charge only 20% upfront to begin work. The remaining 80% is due only after successful reinstatement. We only win when you win" (V, FAQ JSON-LD). "Simple cases: 3–5 days. Standard suspensions: 7–14 days. Section 3 ... 2–4 weeks" (V). The reinstatement page is the careful one: "there is no fixed public price", "Amazon controls its review timing", "Past outcomes are individual examples, not a guaranteed result or a published overall success rate" (V).
- **How they get clients.** Fiverr (6 of 10 testimonials), Upwork (4 of 10), a WhatsApp line "24/7", a free audit form answered "within 2 hours", a 45-minute Google Meet (V).
- **The Fiverr side says what the website avoids.** Search snippets of the linked Fiverr profile: "98% success rate", "2000+ cases handled", "$5M+ recovered", 5.0 from 11 reviews (V as snippets; the page itself blocked the fetch). The website says "94 documented cases". Both cannot be the case count (V).
- **Trust devices, checked one by one.** The "Amazon Authorised Partner" badge links to a Seller Central Service Provider Network page in the _Advertising Optimization_ category, which redirects to sign-in; SPN is a free directory, not a reinstatement credential (V). "Trusted by leading brands" shows four logos with no stated relationship (V). Trustpilot: a claimed profile, 0 reviews, location shown as Spain (V). Ten unique testimonials shown twice to make twenty, all five stars, no dates (V). "94" case studies: 26 PDFs with near-duplicate pairs (V). Market counts: "14 marketplaces", "12+ markets" listing 13, "25+ countries" (V). Ten blog posts all dated 22 Jul 2026 (V). Counters on the testimonials page render as 0 (V).
- **Through the first-60-seconds lens (E).** Better than us: one next step everywhere (WhatsApp, audit, call), registry numbers linked, a real face, downloadable case files, a reinstatement page that admits there is no fixed price and no published rate. Worse: four names, 15 vs 22, 94 vs 2000+, four market counts, counters at 0, repeated reviews, a badge that lands on a login wall, no price before a WhatsApp chat, "80% after results" on the home page beside "no guarantee" on a sub-page, "Since 2019" against a 2025 registration, "24/7 WhatsApp" (the channel our own scam check names as a signal). The lesson is not "do what they do": every number on their site can be checked against a registry or against another number on the same site, and several fail. A one-person software page with four true numbers beats a 22-name page with four unmatched ones.

**What Pakistani sellers hear (V, Seller Forums):** "Many many sellers have been suspended for life for using VA's"; "sellers in Pakistan are being targeted by scammers ... the scammers use your account right up until it gets suspended"; "UK accounts with Pakistani details" sent to a verification dead end; an Amazon staff reply asking for the notification, the supply chain and whether the documents match the account. A tool that never logs in and sends nothing to Amazon answers the first fear directly, and Amazon's own reply lists the questions the decoder already asks.

**What a credible team page looks like for a one-person software company (V).** Staffed agencies show roles, not verifiable people (Thompson & Holt names no staff and prints "100% Success Rate" with exclusions; a "top-10" page ranks its own publisher first with "97.2%"; Krolog says "No legitimate service can guarantee reinstatement" beside "sure-shot recovery solutions"). Trusted small software companies do the opposite: Plausible names both founders and when each joined, "no outside investors", "never spent a cent on advertising", lists what it will not do; Buttondown: "We are funded by our customers", "we'll never force you to deal with an AI-powered chatbot before talking to a human", a status page and a changelog linked. Applied to AppealDeck's /about (F-10, X-13, G-47): one name, one country, the Hawlton trading name, what the software reads and what it never touches, the named processors the privacy policy already lists, the dated test results, who answers support and the window, one real photo or none, no counters, no roster, no badge; every line checkable against a registry, a test file or a policy page, which is the test Dastan's page fails.

## 4. What makes sellers, VAs and writers stay and pay

The findings, the treasure test and the S-01 to S-20 list are in the master report's section 4 (they were written there first). The evidence base:

- **Praise clusters (V):** a named person ("Chris was present, quick to reply" 31 Jul 2026; "I would give Holly 100 stars"; "Thank you Chirag and Team"); kept informed ("he was always checking in, following up" 12 Aug 2026; "kept me informed every step of the way" over 5.5 months, 17 Sep 2026); speed ("resolved the issue within 24 hrs"; "reinstated in 8 days"); persistence ("wrote no fewer than 5 appeals and the last one worked"; "took 6 months ... never gave up"); honesty ("made no promises"); a clear plan with the documents explained. Price is almost never mentioned; none of Riverbend's ten most recent five-star reviews names what was paid.
- **Complaint clusters (V):** ghosted ("I stopped hearing from them altogether for nearly two weeks" 17 Aug 2026; "disappeared for a while, not replying on WhatsApp or by email"); template ("all it was template emails" 26 May 2026; "the same letter for the different persons"; "For $300+ you get a generic 4-5 sentences"); ChatGPT ("clearly AI-generated (literally ChatGPT prompts)" 10 Jun 2025); paid and nothing ("No appeals were ever filed"; "$1,800 up front and do absolutely nothing"); refund ("only half returned"; "I asked refund, they ignore me"). Getida: "Amazon started doing what they are doing automatically - no need to pay 25%". Mr Jeff: Trustpilot "rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines". Not found: a review saying "asked for my login" (the login hazard shows in Fiverr gig asks and forum warnings, not reviews).
- **First-session patterns (V):** Keepa shows the chart on the Amazon page itself, "no account is required to start"; sellerboard: "Demo account", "No registration required!", "No credit card required"; Linear ships a demo workspace ("Changes are local to your browser and reset on refresh"); Helium 10's most consistent complaint is "overwhelming for new users due to the sheer number of features"; Jungle Scout's praised first touch is the estimator's "realistic monthly revenue ranges". The pattern: the finished thing is shown with no sign-up and no card, and calm beats breadth.
- **The "worth it" moment (V):** further rounds included until it worked; a plan and the documents explained; speed; a person who did not disappear. The cheap end: a Gumroad POA template, "no refunds", "not guaranteed to work", inauthentic-goods only.
- **Coming back without a subscription (V):** cases run "nearly three months", "5.5 months", "6 months", "5 appeals"; the return visits are Amazon's reply, the next round and the record; nothing in the reviews praises a dashboard.
- **D7 evidence (V):** Riverbend Guardian "$17/day", monthly, "Cancel anytime", human-led with "75+ marketplace specialists"; AppealsPro monitoring at $199/mo; AmzMonitor $12/mo "without ever touching your Seller Central"; AMZAlert per-ASIN pricing that does not monitor account health; Helium 10 Alerts cut to 5 ASINs ("crappified"); Amazon monitors account health free (Seller Assistant, Account Health Assurance); no review or thread of a seller paying a post-reinstatement watch found (U).
- **Telling others (V):** the UK Seller Forums thread "Should I pay £3,200?": top reply (160 upvotes) says skip the paid service, "the web is full of scammers"; next (110) says post the full notice first; the only recommendation is a named person with a count; sellersasksellers' moderator bans provider promotion. What gets warned about: guarantees, lawyer-equivalence claims, upfront fees, silence, fake reviews.
- **VAs and writers (V):** job posts ask a VA to "Monitor account health dashboard daily", handle "documentation requests", keep a compliance-document library; one employer rejects "Applicants who rely entirely on ChatGPT for POAs"; agencies keep a "suppression log with follow-up date" and run "10-15 brands per pod" with "daily check-ins + weekly summaries; biweekly client decks"; agency tooling sells the client-facing output (AgencyAnalytics "$79 / month, which includes 5 clients"; Shopkeeper "our data wrapped in your brand"). AppealDeck today: no case-name field anywhere in `src`; cases titled by kind and hash; exports are a `.txt` with content hashes; zero copy addressed to VAs or writers; the fact lock has no visible stamp; the pack is one tab away.

### A8. Platform competition and existing support

[Amazon's Seller Assistant announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/innovation-at-amazon/seller-assistant-agentic-ai) advertises account-health analysis, recommended actions and document/compliance assistance, with US-store access at no additional charge. It describes both available and rolling-out capabilities; this research did not test them in a suspended account. [Amazon moderator support guidance](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/8eaba5b9-9edb-4c07-a4b6-e55ed32bce3c?mons_sel_locale=en_US) also identifies an Account Health support channel, subject to account availability. These are meaningful alternatives to our product. Benchmark actual seller tasks against them; do not presume that every seller lacks guidance or that every announced feature is fully available.

## Important unknowns

- No validated count of addressable suspended sellers, conversion rate, acquisition cost or price elasticity. No legitimate basis for projected reinstatement odds.
- No permissioned corpus of 10,000 successful appeals. Training/evaluation rights and outcome selection bias would still need resolution if examples became available.
- No authenticated review of the current US notice/form for every proposed protocol. Every shipped playbook needs this, a named reviewer and a review date.
- No live Paddle approval/payout test, public deployment, production webhook smoke test or deliverability verification established by this research.
- No legal determination of the founder's entity/tax obligations or whether particular advisory features constitute regulated services in a customer jurisdiction.
- No evidence that auto-detection can authenticate invoices, identify competitor abuse or reveal Amazon's internal links, reviewers or automated scoring.

## Evidence hierarchy for implementation

Store the notice and form instructions as case evidence, alongside current marketplace-specific official policy. Do not let one silently override a contradiction in the other. Surface conflicts for review. A dated Amazon moderator explanation is supporting guidance; competitor content is market observation; seller anecdotes are research leads. None should become an uncited mandatory task just because an LLM retrieves it.

# Recruitment Kit — hiring appeals expertise without getting scammed, cloned, or banned

**Why this file exists / when to use it:** AppealDeck's founder is not an Amazon-appeals expert and the AI assistant's domain knowledge needs human validation — so the project must buy appeals expertise from a market that is itself full of exaggerated claims, recycled boilerplate, and (documented by federal prosecutions) outright bribery services. This file is the complete hiring system: which candidate archetypes to pursue in what order, the 20-minute audition that separates real experts from pretenders, the proof standards that work without anyone leaking confidential data, the instant disqualifiers, the platform-compliance rules that keep our Fiverr/Upwork accounts alive, the staged IP-disclosure ladder, and the community bounty program for collecting real approved POAs. Use it from Week 2 (first auditions) through Week 5 (consultant review done). [source: The Second Opinion.md, recruitment kit; STREAM_10_TALENT_OUTSOURCING_FREE_CHEAP.md]

**Terms used here:** POA = Plan of Action, the appeal document Amazon requires (Root Cause → Corrective Actions → Preventive Measures). Section 3 = the clause of Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement cited in the most severe deactivations (often inauthenticity/fraud suspicion). SOP = standard operating procedure. VA = virtual assistant. NDA = non-disclosure agreement. Rev-share = revenue share. Fixture notice = a realistic fake deactivation notice from our test corpus. ASGTG = "Amazon Seller Performance — ASGTG", the largest suspension-focused Facebook community. LLM = large language model. On-platform = paid and messaged entirely inside Fiverr/Upwork's own systems.

**Budget for the whole program:** ~$500–1,200 (estimate): 5–8 paid auditions at $15–50 each, one big-name scoped review at ~$500–2k (partially overlapping budget — see §1 rank 5), first month of any retainer. Every candidate contact is founder-approved before sending (decision rights: `./01-ROLES-AND-OWNERS.md` §2).

## 2. The 20-minute fixture audition (the core vetting mechanic)

Every appeals-expertise candidate — ranks 1–4, no matter the sourcing channel or how strong the profile looks — takes the same test before any hire. It costs $15–50 per candidate as a paid on-platform gig order and filters most weak applicants in 20 minutes; run 5–8 candidates, expect to hire 2–3 [source: STREAM_10_TALENT_OUTSOURCING_FREE_CHEAP.md; The Second Opinion.md].

**The test:** send a fixture Section 3 deactivation notice from our corpus and ask for a POA outline plus the questions they would ask the seller. The fixture contains **two planted traps**:

1. **An ambiguous appeal window** — the notice implies a deadline without stating it cleanly. A real expert flags the ambiguity and states how they'd pin the actual date; a pretender invents a confident number.
2. **A missing invoice fact** — the case cannot be answered honestly without a supplier invoice the notice never mentions and the candidate was never given. **Fabricating the invoice (or writing as if it exists) = instant fail.** **Asking for it = top marks.** This trap is the whole ethics screen in miniature: we are hiring people who will sit near a product that must never help fabricate evidence (ethics spine D6, `../00-DECISION/02-DECISION-LOG.md`).

**Scoring rubric (pass needs all four):**

| Criterion | Pass looks like | Fail looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Trap 1 — deadline | Ambiguity flagged; verification path stated | Confident invented deadline |
| Trap 2 — invoice | Asks for the missing document | Fabricates or assumes it |
| Structure | Root cause → corrective action → preventive measure, mapped 1:1 (each root cause has its own corrective and preventive line) | Generic apology letter; unmapped lists |
| Tone | Zero emotion, zero blame of Amazon, zero legal threats | Pleading, indignation, "our lawyers…" |

Keep the audition text identical across candidates so scores compare. Log every result (candidate, source channel, scores, verdict) in a recruitment sheet — the same sheet feeds action item 6 in `./01-ROLES-AND-OWNERS.md`'s weekly review.

- The fixture notice used for auditions must be one the AI assistant retires from the product test corpus once auditions begin (answers will circulate among freelancers eventually).

## 3. Proof-without-confidential-data standards

Candidates claiming past wins must prove them WITHOUT leaking their clients' or their own confidential data — and we must never ask for more. Acceptable proof formats (any one suffices alongside a passed audition; all three for rank-4 hires):

| Format | What we accept | What must be redacted |
|---|---|---|
| Blurred approval email | Screenshot showing ONLY the reinstatement line + date | Everything else: names, ASINs, case IDs, marketplace, amounts |
| Appeal-history screen recording | A short recording of their own Seller Central appeal-history page showing submitted→approved states | Any client-identifying detail; own-account only unless written client consent exists |
| Reference call | One 15-minute call with a past client the candidate nominates | We take no notes containing the reference's business identifiers |

A candidate who cannot produce ANY of the three formats has no verifiable track record — they can still be hired for structure/SOP work at rank-2 rates, never presented (internally or externally) as an appeals expert.

## 4. Instant disqualifiers (any one ends the conversation)

1. **Success-rate claims above ~90% without evidence** — no honest operator in this market has that number, and publishing invented win rates is exactly what our own ethics spine forbids.
2. **"I can win any appeal / any category"** with no per-type proof — appeals differ radically by violation class; universal claims signal ignorance or dishonesty.
3. **LLM boilerplate in the audition** — if the test answer is generic AI output, they add nothing the product doesn't already do.
4. **Refusing all three proof formats of §3** while still claiming a track record.
5. **ANY offer of insider Amazon access** — "I know someone inside Seller Performance", leaked annotations, internal escalation contacts, account "flag removal". This is not a gray area: the founder of ASGTG — the largest seller-suspension community — pleaded guilty in the federal bribery prosecutions of Amazon insiders (DOJ cases, 2020 indictments → 2022–23 sentencings), and press reporting in Jun 2026 describes a still-active shadow bribery market. **Operating rule: recruit IN that community, never THROUGH its founder — and treat any insider offer as a report-and-block, not a negotiation.** One insider-tainted hire could end the company (payment providers and the CWS both terminate over fraud adjacency).

Also disqualifying for ongoing roles (not instant, but resolve before hire): refusing confidentiality terms, or insisting on off-platform payment for platform-sourced work (§5).

## 5. Outreach & platform compliance (protects our accounts and theirs)

Every rule here exists because breaking it risks a platform ban — for us or the candidate — at exactly the moment we depend on these platforms for talent.

1. **Paid auditions are placed as on-platform gig orders** (Fiverr order / Upwork fixed-price contract). Never "do a free test" (poor-quality selection + banned on r/forhire), never paid off the books.
2. **Ongoing retainers stay on-platform** (Fiverr Subscriptions / Upwork retainer) **or** move off-platform only via the platform's official conversion process — or run the advisor search on non-marketplace channels from the start (LinkedIn, community, referrals).
3. **Rev-share is NEVER pitched inside Fiverr messaging.** Fiverr's off-platform-payment policy treats revenue-share/equity offers in chat as circumvention; it can kill both accounts. Rev-share and advisor terms are discussed only off-marketplace with candidates sourced off-marketplace, or after an official conversion.
4. **Every outreach message is founder-approved before sending, per contact** (`./01-ROLES-AND-OWNERS.md` §2). Three outreach templates exist (big-name consultant / freelancer audition / community bounty) [source: The Second Opinion.md]; the AI assistant drafts, the founder sends from their own named accounts.
5. **Platform cost realities** (plan rates accordingly): Fiverr takes 20% of the order (a $15 audition nets the candidate $12 — price auditions so the candidate's net respects their time); Upwork clients pay a 3–5% marketplace fee; Reddit r/forhire requires account age ≥7 days, ≥25 karma, one post per 7 days, and a stated real rate (quote the audition fee flat — "DM for pricing" gets auto-removed) [source: STREAM_10_TALENT_OUTSOURCING_FREE_CHEAP.md].

## 6. IP staging — what a candidate may see, and when

Disclosure follows a three-stage ladder. No stage is skipped, and paper is understood realistically: an NDA against a Pakistani freelancer is practically unenforceable (arbitration costs more than the secret is worth) — **paper sets norms; architecture stops thieves** [source: The Second Opinion.md; STREAM_7_FOUNDER_PARTNERSHIP_RISK.md §2].

| Stage | When | What is disclosed | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Public pitch** | First contact, auditions | Only what the future landing page says: what AppealDeck is, roughly how it works. The audition itself reveals nothing (fixture notice is fake) | None |
| **2 — Mutual NDA** | Passed audition, moving toward a role; big-name consultant before any review | Product demo, template STRUCTURE (section skeletons, not prompt text), the review scope | Mutual NDA |
| **3 — Advisor agreement** | Ongoing role (retainer, rev-share, bench) | Working materials needed for the role, least-privilege | Advisor agreement per `../01-PHASE-0-BLOCKERS/02-COLLABORATOR-POLICY.md`: confidentiality + **12-month non-solicit** + **contributed-IP assignment** (everything they contribute is assigned to the founder on creation) + the rev-share terms |

**NEVER disclosed at ANY stage, to anyone outside the company:** the prompt templates, the violation-taxonomy logic, and raw outcome data. These live server-side only — a contractor reviews *outputs*, never the machinery. This is the architecture that makes the weak paper acceptable.

Clone realism: the big consulting firms shipped no software in a decade, and SellerForge exists regardless of anything we disclose — **speed is the moat**, not secrecy theater [source: The Second Opinion.md]. Staging exists to avoid handing a ready-to-run kit to the one motivated copier, not to slow ourselves down.

## 7. Community bounty — buying real approved POAs, cleanly

To enrich the exemplar corpus with genuinely approved appeals, run a small bounty in seller communities. Every safeguard below is mandatory — this program touches other people's account data and community rules simultaneously (community mechanics and named-founder conduct: `../05-PHASE-4-GROWTH/02-COMMUNITY-PLAYBOOK.md`).

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Consent-gated | Contributor explicitly consents in writing to our use; contribution form states the use plainly |
| Moderator-cleared | The bounty post is approved by the community's moderators BEFORE posting — an unapproved commercial post gets the founder's community identity banned, which is a strategic asset (D10 distribution) |
| Price | $15–25 per approved POA (an approved POA = one Amazon actually accepted, shown by the reinstatement line + date, §3 standards) |
| Cap | 20 per person — prevents farming and keeps corpus diversity |
| Ownership | Own-account POAs only; a POA written for a client requires the client's written consent attached |
| Anonymization | Contributor submits anonymized text only; we run **founder-side re-redaction anyway** (never trust inbound redaction — scan for names, ASINs, case IDs, addresses, amounts) |
| License | Contributor grants an explicit **non-exclusive license** — they keep their own document; we get the right to use it for product development |
| Data minimalism | Collect the minimum contributor data needed to pay them (GDPR minimalism); no marketing use of contributor identity |

Budget: at $15–25 × a target of 40–60 exemplars, the bounty is roughly $600–1,500 if fully bought — but ranks 3 (barter sellers) and the design-partner program (`../04-PHASE-3-LAUNCH/03-DESIGN-PARTNER-BETA.md`) supply many exemplars for free; buy only the gap.
